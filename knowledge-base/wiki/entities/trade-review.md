# Trade Review

## Role

Trade Review was a Q4 2023 capability associated with trade-detail requirements, exception integration, confirmation documents, and settlement-related notifications.

## Q4 2023 status

Enhancement requirements for the trade detail page were being collected on 2023-11-20. The underlying user stories were scheduled for walkthrough with Dinesh on 2023-11-27.

The planned scope also included integration with Exception Ticket (ET), implementation of CDUPS confirmation documents in Trade Blotter, and application-onboarding support for CDUPS and SSI+.

The source does not provide final requirements approval or implementation evidence.
