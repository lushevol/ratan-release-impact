# eBBS RTA

## Role

eBBS RTA is identified as a possible integration dependency for automatic Delivery versus Payment (DVP).

## Open requirement

The checklist asks whether a new automatic DVP requirement should be implemented and whether integration with eBBS RTA is needed. It does not confirm an approved design, interface contract, or delivery status.
