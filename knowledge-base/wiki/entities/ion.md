# ION

ION is a trade-booking source system included in the clearing-trade scope matrix.

The source states that the first version of an ION trade has clearing status and that the relevant flow is marked for novation to a Clearing House. It does not establish how the status is transmitted to RATAN or whether all ION payments have the same clearing-related risk profile.
