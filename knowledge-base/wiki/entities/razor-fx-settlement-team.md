# Razor FX Settlement team

## Role

The Razor FX Settlement team is identified as a stakeholder for UAT support involving FX replication and settlement behavior.

## Onboarding implication

FX onboarding should include this team in settlement-flow validation and UAT planning. The checklist does not provide ownership details, test cases, or evidence of completed UAT.
