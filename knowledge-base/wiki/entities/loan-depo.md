# Loan Depo

## Role in Prime UK UAT

Loan Depo is a new product identified for onboarding in the FMRP Prime UK UAT Drop 2 checklist.

## Required onboarding coverage

The checklist requires Loan Depo validation for:

- SSI auto-attachment and correct CFI-code capture.
- Settlement-method selection and Nostro auto-stamping.
- SWIFT and accounting generation.
- Pending Fixing STP/NSTP configuration.
- Visibility in the Cashflow Blotter and Dashboard GUI, including Quick Search and the horizontal bar.

The source does not establish that Loan Depo inherits all IRS or CCS lifecycle, fixing, netting, or allocation behavior. Those behaviors require product-specific confirmation.
