# MTS

MTS is a downstream messaging validation point in the manual-entity settlement UAT evidence.

Bahrain cases 22–23 and Qatar cases 22–23 were reported as routed to and successfully processed in MTS. Ghana cases 17–18 were not received in MTS US after AMH acknowledgement, requiring retesting. MTS evidence must be assessed separately from AMH acknowledgement and displayed cashflow status.

See [[mts-downstream-settlement-validation]] and [[amh-acknowledgement-versus-downstream-delivery]].
