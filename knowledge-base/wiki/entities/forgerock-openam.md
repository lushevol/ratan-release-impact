# ForgeRock OpenAM

ForgeRock OpenAM is the documented current OIDC provider for Ratan SSO. It is part of the ForgeRock/OneMFA/OneDS estate that the source says is scheduled for full decommission by the end of Q3 2026.

The deprecated proposal also names FMAA as an Entra outage fallback. Its compatibility with the claimed decommission timeline is unproven.
