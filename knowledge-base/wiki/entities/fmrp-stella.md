# FMRP Stella

## Role

The cashflow-integration source describes FMRP Stella as the proposed pre-trade cashflow-generation application in the FMRP operating model.

The RATAN interface source identifies FMRP STELLA as the booking and trade-processing platform targeted by RATAN interface 29126. That source also uses the names **SABRE**, **Stella API**, **STELLA booking engine**, and **SABRE/STELLA SDK Booking API**.

These descriptions come from different source documents and should not be treated as a single approved implementation contract.

## Responsibilities

### Cashflow generation and lifecycle boundary

The cashflow-integration source assigns Stella responsibility for:

- Generating `New`, `Amendment`, and `Cancellation` cashflow events from trade business events.
- Generating `Business Version` values for trade amendments and withdrawals.
- Defaulting newly generated cashflows to `Projected`.
- Generating theoretical and known cashflows and sending them to [[ratan]].
- Replicating selected post-trade statuses, particularly `Released` and `Settled`, back to Stella-owned views.

Under that proposed operating-model split:

- Netting and suppression are proposed to move out of Stella.
- Post-trade lifecycle management is proposed to move out of Stella.
- Operational payment-date changes are proposed to move out of Stella.

### Booking and trade-processing integration

The RATAN interface source documents the following responsibilities for FMRP STELLA:

- Supplying spot rates for settlement processing.
- Supporting BCS fixed/floating-leg netting checks.
- Receiving cashflow and trade-status write-back from RATAN.
- Receiving confirmation and settlement workflow events.
- Providing trade validation and trade-lock operations.
- Receiving Economic Affirmation (`E0`) events.
- Returning an ACK or NACK after booking-engine processing.

## Integration surface

The RATAN interface source identifies two components of the shared `sabre-booking-api` SDK:

- `StellaBookingApi` — cashflow and trade-status write-back.
- `StellaBookingRestApi` — trade-lock status retrieval.

The event path uses SCBML over a dedicated Kafka topic before processing by the Stella booking engine.

## Date boundary

The cashflow-integration source states that Stella retains the original `Value Date`, while Ratan manages the additional operational `Payment Date`. This separation is intended to prevent post-trade processing from mutating the business date generated by Stella.

## Naming boundary

The RATAN interface source does not formally define whether SABRE is the product family, STELLA is the service or booking platform, and FMRP is the programme or domain context. These names should not be treated as interchangeable without corroborating architecture documentation.

## Source status

The cashflow-integration source is a proposal and does not establish an approved implementation contract.
