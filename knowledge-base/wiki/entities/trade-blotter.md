# Trade Blotter

## Role

Trade Blotter was a Q4 2023 product area for trade visibility, notifications, review, exception handling, and settlement-related controls.

## Q4 2023 scope

The analysis covered:

- Auto Notification in Trade Blotter with Filter under `RATAN-14586` / `Feature 1838136`.
- Trade Review requirements for enhancements to the trade detail page.
- Integration of Trade Review with Exception Ticket (ET).
- ECO affirmation status updates from RATAN to CDU PS.
- CDUPS confirmation-document presentation in Trade Blotter.
- Application onboarding support for CDUPS and [[entities/ssi-plus]].
- Analysis of a value-date range condition in Trade Blotter quick search.
- Verification of data-entitlement enhancements.

The source records requirements collection and testing activity but does not establish final delivery status for every capability.
