# S2B NG

S2B NG is a proposed integration channel for eligible-client automation. The workflow inventory associates it with payment affirmation, payment release, settlement-instruction automation, and full automation of selected netting workflows.

Eligibility, onboarding standards, control ownership, and operational exception handling are not defined by the source.
