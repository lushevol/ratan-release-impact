# Murex G2000

Murex G2000 is identified as the legacy Derivatives platform from which the business is being migrated under [[strategic-fm-re-platforming-sfmrp]].

[[ratan]] is described as supporting this migration to FMRP strategic stacks. Its EOD capability is also used as a [[murex-eod-proxy]] intended to accelerate Murex G2000 EOD migration.

The source does not specify the Murex G2000 product scope, interfaces, data domains, retirement plan, or migration completion status.
