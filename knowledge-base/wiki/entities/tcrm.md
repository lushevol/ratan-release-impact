# TCRM

TCRM is the approval workflow referenced for Net-to-Gross requests from Netting Clients in the UK and US.

The source requires TCRM triggering and a configurable threshold for approval, but does not define threshold calculation, approval outcomes, or sequencing relative to [[nstp]] routing in [[ratan]].
