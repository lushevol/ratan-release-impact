# F2B

F2B is a delivery workstream in the 2025 [[fmrp]] cash-settlement backlog. The source organizes F2B work around product and regional go-lives, migration, shared capabilities, continuous testing, and maintenance.

## Planned Milestones

The source gives planned release windows for:

- CN Loan Depo: January, ADO Feature 6469299.
- UK Prime PM: February, ADO Feature 6469316.
- UK Prime Rates: April, ADO Feature 6469341.
- CN CCS Trade Migration: Q1, ADO 6469344.
- UK E-Precious: Q2, with no ADO identifier shown.

Trade migration is explicitly marked as required for UK Prime Rates, CN CCS, and UK E-Precious. It is marked `NA` for CN Loan Depo and UK Prime PM.

Other F2B items include FXO, Global Rates, Central Entitlements, Keystone (Hongkong), Continuous Testing, Build & Maintain, Strategic CPN, FXU, CLS, and LOANIQ Day2. Most of these rows do not provide complete scope or scheduling information.

## Status Qualification

These entries represent planned backlog scope. They do not demonstrate that a release or go-live occurred. Actual outcomes are tracked as an open question in [[which-2025-f2b-milestones-actually-went-live]].

The source also narrows the planned UK Prime PM window to February, but it does not resolve the exact-date question in [[what-was-the-uk-prime-pm-go-live-date]].
