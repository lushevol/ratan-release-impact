# S2BNG

S2BNG is a cash-settlement platform covered by the shared netting requirements in the Netting Story Board.

It shares with [[ratan]] the requirements for FMO selection of two or more cashflows, ad hoc netting, eligibility validation, pre-release netting controls, Net-to-Gross TCRM workflow for UK and US clients, client configuration, affirmation notifications, net cashflow visibility, audit history, and un-netting/re-netting.

Ratan-only functions—including GTSS ingestion, NSTP routing, TLM matching, CLS netting, and LCM inter-entity netting—are not asserted for S2BNG by this source.

S2BNG should not be assumed to be an alias of [[s2bx]] without authoritative confirmation.
