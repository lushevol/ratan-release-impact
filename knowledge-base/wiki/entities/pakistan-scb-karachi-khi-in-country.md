# Pakistan SCB Karachi KHI (In Country)

Pakistan SCB Karachi KHI (In Country) is a manual settlement entity in the Settlement Day 2 enablement scope.

Its documented UAT validates selected settlement-message routing and exception-handling scenarios through [[fmsgw]], with [[ratan]] as the inbound source and [[amh]] as the downstream recipient. See [[pakistan-manual-entity-settlement-uat]] for the country-specific test coverage.

The available record marks all documented cases as passed, but it omits Test Case 5 and contains incomplete attribution and traceability for some cases.
