# FMRP_ENT_DBF

`FMRP_ENT_DBF` is a Murex 2.11 FMRP record-retention object.

The DOI requires permanent retention of its data. Any amendment requires a change ticket and participation in Murex 2.11 Pre-CAB. This retention rule is distinct from the one-month post-release retention of [[scb-fmrp-dbf]].
