# PRDS

PRDS is the bank-standard product definition adopted by [[entities/stella]] for its China rollout. In the current tactical model, Stella performs product enrichment using PRDS.

The source states that the resulting product mapping is standardized and shared with settlement teams. Enrichment is expected to migrate to [[entities/stamp]].
