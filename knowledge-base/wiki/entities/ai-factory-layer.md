# AI Factory Layer

The AI factory layer is an unnamed proposed integration component in the Email Affirmation Automation requirement. Its response is intended to drive automatic settlement processing in [[ratan]] after a client replies to an affirmation email.

Its architecture, interface, decision authority, identity-validation capabilities, confidence scoring, and error-handling responsibilities are not specified. The source does not establish that this layer may autonomously authorize release of a cashflow; [[ratan]] remains the system expected to perform the release action.

Its required controls are tracked in [[what-controls-validate-ai-mediated-email-affirmation-before-cashflow-release]].
