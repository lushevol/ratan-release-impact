# RATAN-10123

RATAN-10123 is the JIRA issue cited as the context for an SSI+ and Murex 2.11 Vostro SSI analysis.

The referenced analysis reports apparent duplicate and near-duplicate Vostro SSI records in SSI+ China samples and asserts product-catalogue alignment between [[murex-2-11]] and [[ssi-plus]]. It records findings but no explicit remediation, consolidation decision, or authoritative uniqueness rule.

The issue is available at <https://jira.global.standardchartered.com/browse/RATAN-10123>.
