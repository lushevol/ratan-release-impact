# Strategic FM Re-platforming (SFMRP)

Strategic FM Re-platforming (SFMRP) is the initiative under which [[ratan]] is being built. The application profile describes its relevant objective as migrating the Derivatives business from [[murex-g2000]] to FMRP strategic stacks.

The source does not define SFMRP governance, delivery scope, milestones, ownership, target dates, or the specific services comprising the FMRP strategic stacks.
