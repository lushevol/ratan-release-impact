# SFX

## Role

SFX was an operational and disaster-recovery dependency in the Q4 2023 settlement readiness plan.

## Q4 2023 activities

The SFX workstream supported:

- Cashflow lifecycle testing.
- Testing sample data with the LMS process.
- Trade checks through to CDU.
- SIT-environment upgrades with DevOps.
- Lifecycle-test result checking.

On 2023-11-17, RATAN was reported as ready for DR, while the next program-management step remained pending. DR was planned for March 2024, with a target change in May 2024.

## Status limitation

The source does not provide final DR approval or evidence that the planned dates were achieved.
