# Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC)

**Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC)** is used in the source as the institution that opens a USD account at **Citibank (USA)** for cross-border USD settlement.

From ICBC’s perspective, the account is a [[nostro-account]]. From Citibank’s perspective, the same account is a [[vostro-account]].

The source provides no further profile of ICBC and does not establish any RATAN integration involving the bank.
