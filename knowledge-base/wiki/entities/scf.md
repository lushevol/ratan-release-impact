# SCF

SCF is a product family represented by dedicated CN Drop 2 UAT scenarios for:

- Yearly PL sweep with payment suppression
- Compression
- XVA-Premium
- XVA-Manual
- Funding
- AGENCY_FEE
- Generic cashflows

The source provides sample trade IDs for these scenarios but does not provide complete execution outcomes. SCF evidence should remain distinct from the IRS, CCS, and NDF scenarios.
