# MDIS

## Role

MDIS is the required email-dispatch channel for settlement-affirmation messages generated by [[entities/cdups]]. The source does not establish whether MDIS is responsible only for transport or also for delivery status, NACK processing, inbound-message correlation, and retries.

## Open integration questions

The authoritative contract between CDUPS and MDIS should define acknowledgement, successful dispatch, delivery failure, partial failure, retry, and response-correlation states.
