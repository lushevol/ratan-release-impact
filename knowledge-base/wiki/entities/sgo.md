# SGO

## Role in the checklist

SGO is the subject of a concrete currency-configuration acceptance scenario.

The expected behavior is that SGO generates SWIFT and accounting as SGD, without SWIFT or accounting failure, and that SGO Nostro and Vostro instructions are attached automatically. The checklist does not identify whether SGO is a currency code, product identifier, or another configuration identifier; that classification requires confirmation.
