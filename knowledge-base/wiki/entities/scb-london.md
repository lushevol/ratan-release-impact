# SCB London

SCB London is the business entity described as dealing in the Korean market as a Registered Foreign Institution. Applicable Korean transactions must settle through a dedicated [[entities/rfi-nostro-account|RFI Nostro account]] held with SCB Korea.

The requirement identifies SCB London as the booking-entity context for portfolio-based RFI Nostro selection in [[entities/ratan|RATAN]].
