# SCB Singapore

SCB Singapore is the proposed parent entity for the [[vietnam-ifc-branch]]. The source explicitly distinguishes this model from onboarding the branch beneath [[scb-vietnam]].

The proposal would require a distinct FMID or entity representation for the branch, as well as branch-specific routing, SWIFT, accounting, static-data, GUI, access, and downstream-system configuration.

The source does not contain formal approval of the parent model. The legal and systemic relationship remains tracked in [[is-the-vietnam-ifc-branch-part-of-scb-singapore]].
