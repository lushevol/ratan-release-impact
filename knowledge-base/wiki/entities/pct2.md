# PCT2

PCT2 is identified as the current location of FMID mappings. This is relevant to the proposed strategy identifier for booking entities and counterparties because the requirement also calls for a golden source and cross-application identifier model.

The source does not establish whether PCT2 is the authoritative long-term source, a transitional source, or an insufficient mapping repository.
