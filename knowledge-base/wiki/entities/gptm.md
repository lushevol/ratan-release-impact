# GPTM

GPTM is identified as a planned downstream FMO consumer of centralized Nostro static data. The proposed rollout uses [[ratan]] as the pilot consumer, followed by RAZOR, GPTM, and other FMO systems through [[rdm]].

The source does not provide a detailed GPTM interface, schema, cache policy, or account-number transformation contract.
