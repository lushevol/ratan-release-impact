# OpenSearch Dashboards

OpenSearch Dashboards is the native browser-based data-visibility option identified for [[opensearch]] business data.

The source recommends it for searching and inspecting business data, and links to <https://docs.opensearch.org/latest/dashboards/>. It makes an unqualified statement that the product has no capability difference from Kibana. That statement is not validated for a particular release, plugin set, licensing arrangement, or Cash Settlement requirement, and should not be used as a formal equivalence claim.

Access-control, audit, data masking, and export restrictions for Dashboards are not defined in the source. See [[what-data-entitlement-and-audit-controls-govern-dbeaver-and-opensearch-dashboards-access]].
