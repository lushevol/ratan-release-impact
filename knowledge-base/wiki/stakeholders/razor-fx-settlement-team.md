# Razor FX Settlement Team

The Razor FX Settlement Team is a stakeholder in the FX Replication work identified by the FXO onboarding checklist.

## Responsibilities Identified by the Source

The team must be engaged to provide UAT support for FX Replication. The source does not define the scenarios, environments, evidence requirements, named owner, or delivery dates for that support.

## Involvement

The team is associated with [[razor]] and the onboarding of [[fxo]]. Its UAT role complements the development and UAT dependency assigned to the [[razor-fx-dev-team]].
