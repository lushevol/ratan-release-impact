# Development

## Role in RATAN technical recovery

Development retains ownership of technical recovery design and permanent remediation when PSS performs an approved temporary recovery.

Responsibilities include:

- Root-cause analysis.
- Recovery design.
- Permanent fix delivery.
- Automation.
- Providing a named owner for each tracked exception.
- Committing and managing the permanent-fix ETA.

## Prioritization responsibility

Development must address issues according to the source-defined thresholds. Repeated manual recovery is a signal of [[ratan-resilience-control-debt]] and should be prioritized above normal enhancement backlog work where applicable.

Slippage against an agreed permanent-fix target date must be escalated to the Development Head, PSS Head, and CPO.
