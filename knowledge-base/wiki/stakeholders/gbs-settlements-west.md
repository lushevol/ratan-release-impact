# GBS Settlements West

GBS Settlements West is a settlement-contact routing column in the RATAN operational directory.

The source assigns West contacts at selected profile level, including `FXDOpsWest.Market@sc.com` and `DerivativesSett-CH@sc.com`. The assignment varies by `FMCODE` and `FMID`; it is not a universal country-level rule.
