# NM_COE

## Role

`NM_COE` is identified as the support contact for issues encountered during the NAMS Nostro account-opening process. The guide states that NM_COE can help guide requestors and resolve process issues.

The source provides a shared mailbox, `NM_COE@sc.com`, rather than named individuals or a formal support model.

## Scope boundary

The source does not define:

- Support ownership by issue type.
- Escalation or response-time commitments.
- Whether NM_COE approves account requests.
- Whether NM_COE has authority over Network Manager decisions.
- Technical support responsibilities for downstream TP Systems.
