# TB OPS

TB OPS is the operations function named in the utilization-response exception procedure. When a RATAN NACK indicates an out-of-scope, amended, or cancelled trade, TB OPS is expected to consult FMO and coordinate with the client or Sales to obtain a replacement Contract ID or Contract Number.

The source does not define TB OPS ownership boundaries, escalation targets, or service-level expectations.
