# MO/Business

## Role in technical-failure recovery

MO/Business may perform trade actions required to support recovery, including dummy or amend actions and cancel or rebook actions.

These activities address trade or business-side corrections when needed; they do not replace Development ownership of technical root-cause analysis or permanent remediation.
