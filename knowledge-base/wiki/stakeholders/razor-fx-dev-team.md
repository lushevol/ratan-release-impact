# Razor FX Dev Team

The Razor FX Dev Team is a stakeholder in the FX Replication work identified by the FXO onboarding checklist.

## Responsibilities Identified by the Source

The team must be engaged for:

- Development changes associated with FX Replication.
- UAT support.

The checklist does not name an individual owner, define the required changes, identify a UAT environment, or record target dates and deliverables. The dependency is therefore recognized but not yet actionable as a complete work item.

## Involvement

The team is associated with [[razor]] and the broader onboarding of [[fxo]]. Coordination is also required with the [[razor-fx-settlement-team]].
