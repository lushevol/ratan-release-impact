# OPS Team

The OPS team is an operational stakeholder in the [[cash-settlement-2025-roadmap]]. The temporary technical recovery process source refers to this stakeholder as **Ops**.

## Involvement

The cash-settlement roadmap source warns that UAT cases should receive more careful review from the OPS and [[dev-team]] groups and that both should be more closely involved in delivery progress.

This associates the OPS team with:

- UAT case review
- Operational-readiness validation
- Progress monitoring
- Migration support

## Role in the Temporary Recovery Process

According to the RATAN temporary recovery process, Ops validates replay or recovery outcomes when business confirmation is required.

This validation role does not transfer permanent technical ownership from Development to Ops.

## Scope Boundaries

The RATAN process source specifies the following boundaries:

- [[bcs-flow|BCS Flow]] is explicitly excluded from the temporary [[pss|PSS]] recovery process.
- BCS Flow’s existing Ops replay process remains in place.
- Data issues remain subject to Ops BAU processes.
- Business exceptions remain subject to MO or Business correction processes.
