# FMO Users

FMO Users are the operational users who select cashflows for netting and review netting exceptions.

In Ratan, their maker/checker profiles and limits govern manual release of net cashflows. Across Ratan and S2BNG, they must be able to select at least two cashflows for ad hoc netting and review and accept a replacement net cashflow after an amendment affects an unreleased net.
