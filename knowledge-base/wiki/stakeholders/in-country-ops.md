# In Country Ops

In Country Ops is a contact-routing category for local settlement operations in the RATAN directory.

The source provides local mailboxes for selected profiles, such as Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Tanzania, and Korea. It does not state that every country has an In Country Ops contact or explain the meaning of blank entries.

Korea routing is internally inconsistent in the source. See [[what-is-the-current-korea-settlement-onboarding-and-contact-routing-status]].
