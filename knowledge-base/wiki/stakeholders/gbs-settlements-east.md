# GBS Settlements East

GBS Settlements East is a settlement-contact routing column in the RATAN operational directory.

The source lists regional mailboxes and individual contacts for selected settlement profiles, including `OTCDerivativeSettlementEast@sc.com` and `GBSDerivSettsGCNA@sc.com`. These entries identify routing destinations, not system ownership, approval authority, or settlement lifecycle responsibility.
