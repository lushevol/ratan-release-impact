# Is the Derivative Process Issue Tracker the Authoritative RATAN BAU Issue Register?

## Question

Is the Confluence tracker titled “Tracker -Derivative Process Issue - Formalisation & Process Governance FMO” the authoritative register for RATAN BAU issues and enhancements?

## Known Evidence

[[25-cash-settlement-home-page--25-cash-settlement-home-page--22-functional-requirement--28-02-issue-tracking-tech--6o5vl6]] links to the tracker as an “Issue tracking Link from BAU Team.” It does not reproduce tracker contents or define its authority.

## Information Required

- Confirm the tracker owner, intended scope, and whether RATAN issues are in scope.
- Confirm access controls, update cadence, retention expectations, and issue lifecycle definitions.
- Obtain an export or controlled snapshot containing issue identifiers, statuses, priorities, owners, dates, and linked evidence.
- Determine whether issues are linked to formal change records, including [[chg0988640]] and [[chg1047654]] where relevant.
- Identify the system of record if this tracker is not authoritative.

## Decision Impact

Without an authoritative issue register, the team cannot reliably establish whether a reported issue is current, resolved, duplicated, accepted as technical debt, or remediated through a change or release.
