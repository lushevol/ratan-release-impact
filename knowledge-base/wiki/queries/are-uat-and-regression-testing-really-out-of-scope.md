# Are UAT and Regression Testing Really Out of Scope?

The checklist marks both UAT and regression testing as not required for Tranche 1. No rationale, compensating control, or approval is recorded. This is notable because the same checklist changes routing, SWIFT generation, settlement accounting, SSI hierarchy, and static settlement data.

## Evidence required

Obtain an explicit risk-based decision from Settlement Ops and the Dev Team, including any alternative validation, regression coverage, production monitoring, and rollback plan.
