# Who Owns Each ADO Story Status Transition?

The workflow names BA, DEV, and QA activities but does not assign accountable owners for status changes or approval gates.

## Questions

- Who prioritizes a story and confirms its planning fields?
- Who determines that analysis is sufficient for development?
- Who approves technical design before `In Development`?
- Who validates `Dev Done` evidence and QA-case review?
- Who can place work on hold or resume it?
- Who confirms test completion and any final release or closure state?

Current evidence is limited to the process described in [[story-status-lifecycle]] and [[development-completion-gate]].
