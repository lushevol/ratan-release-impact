# What Is the Approved Inter-Entity Rebook Cashflow Control?

The requirement calls for a clear identifier for rebooked cashflows and an authorization pop-up when users authorize a netted flow. It states that no indicator currently exists and that the solution depends on STP and matching behavior.

Define the identifier, its data source, the authorization warning, whether STP must be blocked or downgraded, and the treatment of rebooked cashflows in [[inter-entity-cashflow-pre-match]].
