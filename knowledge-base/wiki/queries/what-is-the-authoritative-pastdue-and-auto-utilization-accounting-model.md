# What Is the Authoritative PastDue and Auto Utilization Accounting Model?

The MVP text says an unutilized cashflow at value-date EOD should be auto-utilized to FXBRREC. Phase 2 instead proposes moving a residual amount to a separate Past Due Account and later reversing that position before utilization.

The source also describes inconsistent debit/credit directions in example entries. Required resolution includes the release-specific process, account mapping, timing, status effects, and approved accounting conventions.
