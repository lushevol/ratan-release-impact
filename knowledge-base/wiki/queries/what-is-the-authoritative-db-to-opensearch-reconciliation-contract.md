# What Is the Authoritative DB-to-OpenSearch Reconciliation Contract?

The rollout plan identifies missing real-time reconciliation and schedules a reconciliation strategy, but does not define the contract.

Required clarification includes the authoritative system during parallel running, matching identifiers, comparison frequency, acceptable replication lag, treatment of updates and deletions, discrepancy tolerance, alerting, remediation ownership, and release-blocking thresholds.
