# What Is the Authoritative Agency Payment Booking and SWIFT Generation Model?

The session describes agency-profile bookings that may not generate a China agent queue payment or SWIFT message, while Field 72 reportedly identifies agency payments.

The open design question is whether payment identification should remain portfolio-based, be supplemented by Field 72, or use another booking attribute. The booking model, target queue behavior, SWIFT-generation rule, accounting behavior, and Field 20 requirement need confirmation from Front Office and implementation owners.

No live production test case was available when the meeting was recorded.
