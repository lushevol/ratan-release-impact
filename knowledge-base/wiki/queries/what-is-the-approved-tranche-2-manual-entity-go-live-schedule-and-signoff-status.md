# What Is the Approved Tranche 2 Manual-Entity Go-Live Schedule and Signoff Status?

The checklist has no technical Ratan go-live date for Bahrain, Qatar, Uganda, Ghana, or Nigeria. Only Bahrain has an imprecise business target: “Third week of Sepetember.”

The UAT-signoff attachment field is blank for all five countries, while CPT is represented only by external links with no outcome, defects, or approval recorded.

Confirm, for every Tranche 2 entity:

- technical and business go-live dates;
- UAT execution and formal signoff;
- CPT and LMS-verification results;
- open defect and exception status; and
- release approver and authorization record.
