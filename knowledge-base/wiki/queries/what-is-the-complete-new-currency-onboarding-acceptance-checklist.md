# What Is the Complete New-Currency Onboarding Acceptance Checklist?

The available checklist identifies five readiness areas but does not define operational acceptance criteria.

A complete onboarding control should establish:

- required cutoff, Nostro, PM classification, ISO mapping, and rounding fields;
- the legal-entity, branch, portfolio, and currency scope for each configuration;
- accountable owners, approvers, and maker/checker controls;
- defaults and failure handling for missing configuration;
- pre-production test cases and expected SWIFT and Accounting outcomes;
- evidence required before production enablement;
- post-release monitoring and rollback procedures.

It is also unresolved whether the H1 SG/MY/IN tactical rounding configuration applies to all future BAU currencies.
