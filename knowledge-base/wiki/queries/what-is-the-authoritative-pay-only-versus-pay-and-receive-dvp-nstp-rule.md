# What Is the Authoritative Pay-Only Versus Pay-and-Receive DVP NSTP Rule?

The requirement directs that the existing DVP NSTP rule may remain unchanged. Earlier feedback proposed applying the DVP rule only to pay cashflows so receive cashflows can be STP, possibly with country-specific exceptions.

Confirm the production rule semantics, applicable entities, effective date, and governance process. Auto DVP relies on a DVP-held pay leg but should not silently determine the broader NSTP policy.
