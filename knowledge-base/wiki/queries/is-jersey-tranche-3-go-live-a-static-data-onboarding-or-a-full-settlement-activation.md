# Is Jersey Tranche 3 Go-Live a Static-Data Onboarding or a Full Settlement Activation?

## Question

Does the Tranche 3 go-live scope enable full Jersey settlement activity, or is it limited to foundational static-data onboarding?

## Evidence

The checklist is titled as a static-data go-live checklist, but explicitly states that users are not settling trades in Jersey and instructs maintainers to leave its currency list unchanged.

## Information needed

- Approved scope statement and effective date;
- confirmation of whether Jersey can settle trades after release;
- release approval and operational-readiness evidence; and
- any staged activation criteria for currency enablement.
