# Are RATAN Capacity Factors Tested or Only SLA Assumptions?

The source labels its figures as capacity agreed in SLA and links to Post Portal baseline, application-performance, and non-functional-testing materials. It does not reproduce actual measurements, test conditions, test environments, acceptance criteria, or pass/fail outcomes.

The linked Confluence materials must be reviewed to determine:

- which capacity figures were used as test inputs;
- which targets were validated;
- whether [[ratan]] and [[ratanone]] met the stated targets; and
- whether the 5-second maximum and 2-second average [[cashflow-blotter]] loading targets have defined percentiles and test conditions.
