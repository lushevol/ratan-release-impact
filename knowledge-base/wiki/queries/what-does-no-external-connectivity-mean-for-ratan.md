# What Does No External Connectivity Mean for RATAN?

The RATAN application profile records external connectivity as `Not Applicable`. Existing technical documentation nevertheless describes integrations involving systems and services such as EBBS, TLM, OLTP Accounting, ENISIS, RDM, FileIT, Solace, and TDS3.

Determine whether the profile field is a CMDB classification indicating no direct external connectivity, rather than a statement that RATAN has no integrations.

## Evidence Needed

- The definition and permitted values of the CMDB external-connectivity field.
- RATAN's approved integration inventory and boundary classification.
- Confirmation of which integrations are direct, internal, externally mediated, or retired.
