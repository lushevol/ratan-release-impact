# What Is the RATAN Capacity Peak Measurement Interval?

Capacity Factor-2 gives peak assumptions of 19,396 for Settlement, 10,927 for Trade intraday, and 58,713 for Trade EOD. The source describes the interval as “per hour or minutes,” without identifying the interval applicable to each figure.

This must be resolved before comparing peak values with multiplier targets expressed per hour, sizing infrastructure, or determining compliance with SLA capacity.
