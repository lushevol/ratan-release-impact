# What Is the Authoritative CDU Confirmation Status Contract?

The functional requirement refers to CDU confirmation, including confirmation of the latest major version for several Stella event types, but does not define CDU.

Establish:

- CDU's full identity, owner, and authoritative upstream source.
- The status vocabulary and meaning of each status.
- How confirmation is associated with a trade and major version.
- Whether CDU publishes to TDS3, Ratan, or another component.
- Interface payload, timing, replay, correction, and failure-handling rules.
- The authoritative resolution of version mismatches and status regressions.
