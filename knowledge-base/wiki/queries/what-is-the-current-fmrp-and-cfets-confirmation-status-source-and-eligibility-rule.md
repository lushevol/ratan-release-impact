# What Is the Current FMRP and CFETS Confirmation Status Source and Eligibility Rule?

A deprecated requirement says that FMRP and CFETS confirmation statuses should be sourced directly from Stella rather than CDU Lake. It names `AFFIRMED` and `CONFIRMED` for FMRP and includes `COMP` for CFETS.
