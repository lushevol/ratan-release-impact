# When Can a UTIL or Gross Settlement Method Be Amended?

The source requests both gross-to-`UTIL` and `UTIL`-to-gross conversion for hybrid customers, but also requires an all-profile hard block after utilization and reversal before financial amendment.

It remains unclear whether conversion is allowed before utilization only, whether a reversal is always required, whether Razor CnR is temporary or sanctioned, and whether BLADE or RATAN owns final validation.
