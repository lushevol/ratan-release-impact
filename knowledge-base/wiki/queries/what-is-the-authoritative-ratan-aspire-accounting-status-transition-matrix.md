# What Is the Authoritative RATAN Aspire Accounting Status Transition Matrix?

The requirement defines `HOLD`, `DISABLED`, `SUCCESS`, and `MISSING_INFO`, but it does not define every valid transition or every combination with cashflow statuses.

Confirm entry-creation timing, value-date release behavior, `MISSING_INFO` remediation and re-evaluation, reversal behavior after `SUCCESS`, and how component cashflow withdrawals differ from standard withdrawals.
