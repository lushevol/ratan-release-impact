# What Is the Authoritative Murex-Stella Cashflow Reconciliation Horizon?

The source specifies both a next-nine-days cashflow extraction and a `T+7` payment-date scope for the same generation reconciliation. The authoritative horizon, calendar basis, cut-off time, and treatment of non-business days need confirmation.
