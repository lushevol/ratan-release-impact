# What Are the Ratan Key Deliverables and Their Owners?

The available Ratan register is only a template. It defines columns for Application, Owner, ADO ticket, Description, Benefit, Status, Cut-off time, and Comment, but contains no populated entries.

## Questions to Resolve

- Which deliverables must be recorded and which applications do they concern?
- Who is accountable for each deliverable: an individual, a team, an application owner, or a service owner?
- Are ADO tickets the authoritative progress record, and can one deliverable link to multiple tickets?
- Which controlled vocabulary should be used for `Status`?
- What operational meaning and timezone apply to `Cut-off time`?
- Should change or release identifiers such as `CHG...` be captured alongside ADO tickets?
- Who maintains the register, and how frequently is it reviewed?
