# What Is the Authoritative Owner and Review Cadence for RATAN Settlements Ops Contacts?

## Open Question

Who owns the settlement contact matrix, and what review and approval process ensures that its profile mappings and mailboxes remain current?

## Evidence Gap

The source has no stated owner, version, effective date, review cadence, approval record, deprecation policy, or operational SLA. It also leaves blank contact records unexplained.

## Required Resolution

Identify:

1. The accountable business owner for the matrix.
2. The technical or static-data owner for `FMCODE` and `FMID` mapping changes.
3. The required review cadence and evidence of validation.
4. The change-approval and distribution process.
5. The escalation path when a settlement profile has no listed contact.

Until this is resolved, the directory should be treated as a routing reference rather than an authoritative ownership register.
