# What Is the Authoritative RATAN Aspire Static Data Source and Change Governance?

The requirement supplies revised entity, FMID, branch-code, bridge-account, and Nostro-account data without identifying ownership or change controls.

Establish the authoritative runtime source, validation rules, effective dating, approval and audit process, treatment of aliases such as `Jersey` and `JERSEY_BR`, and confirmation of the replacement SCS HK bridge account.
