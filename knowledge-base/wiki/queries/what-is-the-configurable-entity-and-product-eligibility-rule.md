# What Is the Configurable Entity and Product Eligibility Rule?

The proposal requires enrichment only for three entity FM IDs, three foreign-exchange product taxonomies, and grouped cashflows containing exactly two payment currencies. It also requires future entities to be supported through configuration.

The unresolved issue is where these lists and rules are owned, how they are changed, and whether adding an entity requires code deployment.
