# Which Cashflow Blotter Custom Search/View Filters Are Approved for Removal?

The source requires the Product Owner to provide the list of unutilized filters before removal can proceed. The approved filter-removal inventory is therefore unresolved.

## Information required for each candidate

For every proposed filter, confirm:

- display name;
- technical identifier, if available;
- definition of “not utilized”;
- usage evidence and observation period;
- affected roles and user groups;
- impact on saved searches and views;
- business-owner approval; and
- rollback or restoration decision.

The screenshot referenced by the source may help identify the visible interface, but it is not an authoritative inventory.

## Current assessment

Filter removal is not implementation-ready until the Product Owner supplies and approves the list. The alphabetical ordering requirement can be progressed independently, subject to the sorting rules tracked in [[queries/what-are-the-authoritative-alphabetical-sorting-rules-for-cashflow-blotter-filters-and-views]].
