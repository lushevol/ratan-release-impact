# Is the Vietnam IFC Branch Legally and Systemically Part of SCB Singapore?

## Question

Has the proposed [[vietnam-ifc-branch]] been approved as a branch of [[scb-singapore]], rather than part of [[scb-vietnam]], and how will each system represent that relationship?

## Current Evidence

The source clearly proposes a new branch under SCB Singapore and a new FMID or entity. It does not provide formal legal, architecture, regulatory, accounting, or governance approval.

The terms “branch,” “entity,” and “FMID” are used without establishing whether they refer to the same identity.

## Evidence Needed

- Legal-entity and branch approval.
- Architecture decision.
- Regulatory and booking model.
- Authoritative FMID and FM code.
- Accounting branch model.
- SWIFT BIC and branch-code model.
- Entitlement and reporting consequences.
- Named approvers and effective date.

Until this evidence is available, the SCB Singapore parent model should remain marked as proposed.
