# What Do the Two Dates in the RATAN DOI Rules Approval History Represent?

Each of the two documented NSTP approval-history rows contains two dates in a single `Approval Date` field:

- `2023-11-16 2023-11-21`
- `2023-11-17 2023-11-21`

The source does not label the dates. They may represent request, review, or approval dates, but that interpretation cannot be established from the guide.

The DOI record should be corrected or supplemented before these entries are used as authoritative audit evidence.
