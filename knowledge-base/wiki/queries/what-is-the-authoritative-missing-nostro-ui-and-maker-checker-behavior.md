# What Is the Authoritative Missing Nostro UI and Maker/Checker Behavior?

Missing Nostro requirements conflict on the initial Nostro field state. Some descriptions say that a system-assigned Nostro is displayed and non-editable despite the Nostro result being missing. Other descriptions say a Nostro candidate list is presented for manual selection.

Clarification is required for SCB Pay and SCB Receive separately, including whether Vostro can be edited, whether Nostro is selectable by default, what an Edit control changes, and what data is visible after rejection and resubmission.
