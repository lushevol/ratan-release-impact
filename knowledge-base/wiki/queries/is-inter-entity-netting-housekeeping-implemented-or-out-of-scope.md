# Is Inter-Entity Netting Housekeeping Implemented or Out of Scope?

## Question

Is automated housekeeping absent from the release, inherited from existing RATAN infrastructure, or incorrectly documented?

## Contradictory evidence

The questionnaire states:

- Section 3.10: `Housekeeping: NO`, `CRISP: NO`, `ASRM: NO`.
- Section 3.11: `Zero Manual Tasks for BAU`.
- Section 4.5: `All Key Operations Automated`.
- Section 7.7: `Housekeeping: [Detail provided]`.

These statements require qualification. The source does not identify housekeeping jobs, schedules, targets, ownership, remediation, or ASRM evidence.

## Required resolution

Confirm the scope of housekeeping for files, databases, temporary data, logs, and inbound/outbound locations. Identify the implementation or inherited process, its owner and schedule, and any CRISP remediation if the control is missing.
