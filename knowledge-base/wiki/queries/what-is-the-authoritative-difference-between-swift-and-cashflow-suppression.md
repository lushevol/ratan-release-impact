# What Is the Authoritative Difference Between Swift and Cashflow Suppression?

The Sri Lanka hands-on training checklist names Swift Suppression and Cashflow Suppression as separate scenarios. This supports treating them as distinct in that training scope, but does not state whether one triggers, blocks, or is independent of the other.

Required evidence includes the applicable eligibility rules, status transitions, SWIFT-generation behavior, downstream settlement effects, accounting effects, and recovery actions.
