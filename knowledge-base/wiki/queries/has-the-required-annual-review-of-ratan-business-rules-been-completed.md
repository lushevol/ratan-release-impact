# Has the Required Annual Review of RATAN Business Rules Been Completed?

The guide requires the MT or a delegate to review every rule annually and record the review date and reviewer in the DOI. Its Rules Review History table is empty.

The authoritative DOI or governance repository should be checked to determine whether reviews occurred elsewhere, identify overdue rules, and establish the current review owner.
