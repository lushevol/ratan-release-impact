# Which 2025 F2B Milestones Actually Went Live?

## Question

Which planned 2025 [[f2b]] milestones reached production, and what were their actual release or go-live dates?

## Evidence Available

The FMRP requirement backlog records these planned windows:

- CN Loan Depo: January.
- UK Prime PM: February.
- UK Prime Rates: April.
- CN CCS Trade Migration: Q1.
- UK E-Precious: Q2.

It also identifies other F2B work without sufficient scheduling detail, including FXO, Global Rates, Central Entitlements, Keystone (Hongkong), Continuous Testing, Build & Maintain, Strategic CPN, FXU, CLS, and LOANIQ Day2.

## Current Assessment

The source is a planning artifact and supplies no completion status or release evidence. The listed windows cannot be treated as actual go-live dates.

The UK Prime PM evidence supports February as its planned month but leaves [[what-was-the-uk-prime-pm-go-live-date]] unresolved at day-level precision.

## Evidence Needed

Resolution requires one or more of the following:

- Azure DevOps completion and deployment records for the referenced work items.
- Production release notes.
- Change-management or implementation records.
- UAT sign-off followed by production deployment confirmation.
- Operational announcements identifying the effective go-live date.

Results should distinguish planned, entered UAT, released, deferred, cancelled, and unknown statuses.
