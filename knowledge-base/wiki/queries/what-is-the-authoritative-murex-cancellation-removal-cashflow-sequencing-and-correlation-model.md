# What Is the Authoritative Murex Cancellation-Removal Cashflow Sequencing and Correlation Model?

## Question

What authoritative event-ordering and correlation model must [[ratan]] apply to Murex cashflows generated by booking, cancellation, and cancellation removal?

The deprecated source describes a scenario where `C5` and `C6` may arrive before their apparent predecessors, creating duplicate-payment exposure. It also distinguishes reversal-linked pairs (`C1`/`C3` and `C2`/`C4`) from allegedly standalone cancellation-removal cashflows.

## Information required

- The source-of-truth ordering field or fields for Murex market events and cashflows.
- Stable identifiers for trade, market event, predecessor event, cashflow business identity, and lifecycle version.
- Whether cancellation-removal cashflows are new cashflows, reactivations, replacements, reversals, or another lifecycle category.
- Whether apparent missing linkage is present in Murex but omitted by an interface transformation, or absent at source.
- Ratan's idempotency key and duplicate/replay detection rule.
- The allowed out-of-order processing approach: buffering, deterministic version comparison, rejection, reconciliation, or another control.
- Retention and audit-history requirements when the current trade ID does not express the originating event chain.
