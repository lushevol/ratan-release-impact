# Why Is RATAN Trade EOD Capacity Targeted at 2X?

The plan broadly describes RATAN as capable of processing up to four times hourly volume, yet Trade EOD is explicitly calculated at 2X:

`58,713 * 2 = 117,426 per hour`

Settlement and Trade intraday are calculated at 4X. Clarification is needed on whether the Trade EOD exception reflects a business requirement, a technical constraint, a different operating interval, or an incomplete target.
