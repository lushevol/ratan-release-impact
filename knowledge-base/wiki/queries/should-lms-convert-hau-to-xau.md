# Should LMS Convert HAU to XAU?

The HAU onboarding checklist asks whether [[lms]] should convert HAU to [[xau]]. No mapping decision, payload specification, consumer behavior, or reconciliation consequence is documented.

Resolution requires a canonical currency representation for LMS messages and evidence that all downstream consumers can process the selected representation without loss of settlement meaning.
