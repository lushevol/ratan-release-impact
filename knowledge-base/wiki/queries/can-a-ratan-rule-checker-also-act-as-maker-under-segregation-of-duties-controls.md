# Can a RATAN Rule Checker Also Act as Maker Under Segregation-of-Duties Controls?

The guide contains two apparently conflicting statements. Its overview says that a checker can also perform maker actions. The Rule Engine Blotter and cashflow suppression procedures state that the maker and checker should be different people.

The document does not define whether the exception applies to specific actions, user profiles, environments, or approval paths. The formal segregation-of-duties rule and the permitted profile combinations should be confirmed.
