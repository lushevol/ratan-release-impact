# What Is the Approved RATAN Indonesia UAT Scope for MX211, RATAN, FMSGW, LMS, and STELLA/TL?

## Open question

What approved UAT scenarios, interfaces, data requirements, owners, acceptance criteria, and exit conditions apply to MX211, RATAN, FMSGW, LMS, and FMRP - STELLA / TL for the RATAN Indonesia migration?

## Evidence

The source directs teams to follow an unavailable Tranche 2 testing scope but provides no substantive scope under the MX211, RATAN, FMSGW, or FMRP - STELLA / TL headings. LMS is only associated with RATAN Released/Settled test cases.

The related EBBS/TLM statement also assumes FMSGW integration testing without defining FMSGW scenarios.

## Why this matters

Without an approved scenario inventory and measurable acceptance criteria, these systems cannot be reliably included in or excluded from UAT readiness and sign-off.

## Needed resolution

Locate the authoritative Tranche 2 scope and confirm:

1. mandatory functional, integration, negative, and regression scenarios;
2. required execution environments and test data;
3. expected interface events and validation evidence;
4. accountable test owners and sign-off authority; and
5. whether any systems are intentionally out of scope.
