# Are Hefei UAT and Regression Tests Required?

The checklist marks UAT, assigned to Settlement Ops, and regression testing, assigned to Dev Team, as not required. This conflicts with the checklist's required changes to SWIFT generation, strategic routing, settlement accounting, and GUI dropdowns.

## Evidence needed

- The policy or approved risk assessment permitting the test activities to be omitted.
- The accountable owner for test acceptance and production sign-off.
- Evidence of any alternative test execution, such as component, integration, or production-verification testing.
- Defined acceptance criteria for routing, SWIFT messages, accounting postings, and GUI visibility.
