# How Does an On Hold Story Return to Active Work?

The documented `On Hold` process requires a blocker comment and attached evidence, but it does not define resolution, approval, restoration, or escalation rules.

## Evidence needed

- The ADO transition configuration from `On Hold`.
- Examples of resolved blocked stories and their restored statuses.
- Accountabilities for confirming blocker resolution.
- Escalation and reporting expectations for prolonged blocked work.

See [[blocked-story-handling]].
