# What Is the Authoritative eBBS Accounting Entity Scope?

The opening section identifies India, Singapore, Malaysia, China, and Hong Kong as eBBS countries. The embedded posting-branch and Bridge-account mappings cover additional locations, including the United Kingdom, Germany, Mauritius, the United Arab Emirates, Indonesia, the Philippines, Japan, South Africa, the United States, Nepal, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt.

The production scope and the relationship between the country list and static-data coverage require confirmation.
