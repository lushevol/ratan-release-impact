# Are Netting Components Required to Share Currency and Value Date?

The demo requires the resultant amount to equal the sum of its component cashflows, but it does not specify the eligibility attributes for netting.

Clarification is needed on whether components must share currency, value date, settlement account, legal entity, counterparty, or other settlement attributes; how rounding is performed; and whether zero-result netting is permitted.
