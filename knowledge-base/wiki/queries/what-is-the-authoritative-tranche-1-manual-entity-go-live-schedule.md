# What Is the Authoritative Tranche 1 Manual-Entity Go-Live Schedule?

The checklist header defines RATAN Technical, RATAN Business, and ISO go-live dates, but most country rows contain fewer values than the header requires. Vietnam and Zambia contain multiple unlabelled values, and multiple entries omit a year.

## Required resolution

Obtain an authoritative schedule that explicitly records, for each Tranche 1 country:

- RATAN Technical go-live date and timezone.
- RATAN Business go-live date and timezone.
- ISO go-live date and timezone.
- Current status, approval owner, and any superseded dates.

Until this is available, the checklist's dates must be treated as unparsed and non-authoritative.
