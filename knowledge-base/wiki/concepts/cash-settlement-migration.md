# Cash Settlement Migration

Cash settlement migration is the controlled transition of settlement processing for selected trades or cashflows between systems. In this source, Murex2.11 and Stella cashflows feed Ratan Settlement while temporary NSTP and suppression controls isolate migration-related activity.

The process combines scope confirmation, rule configuration, monitoring, cashflow suppression and un-suppression, forward exports, cross-system reconciliation, and restoration of BAU behavior.

The runbook documents intended controls and partial completion comments, but it does not establish final migration success.
