# Annual Business-Rule Review

Annual business-rule review is the governance control requiring the MT or a delegate to reassess every settlement rule at least once a year.

## Required review

The review should confirm that each rule:

- Remains relevant.
- Is still required for effective and efficient settlement processing.
- Continues to meet applicable compliance requirements.

At completion, the reviewer and review date must be recorded in the DOI.

## Evidence gap

The source mandates this review but contains an empty Rules Review History table. It therefore does not demonstrate that the required reviews were completed. The authoritative DOI record and review status should be confirmed.
