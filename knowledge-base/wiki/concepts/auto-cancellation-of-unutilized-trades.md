# Auto Cancellation of Unutilized Trades

Auto Cancellation of unutilized trades is a required FXU workflow. The source does not define eligibility, timing, cancellation authority, notification, interaction with cancellation charges, or exception and reversal behavior.

The workflow must be specified separately from the hard block that prevents settlement-method changes after full or partial utilization.
