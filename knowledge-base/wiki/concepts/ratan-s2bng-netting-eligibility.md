# Ratan/S2BNG Netting Eligibility

The Netting Story Board defines an eligibility rule specific to Ratan and S2BNG. It must not be conflated with [[ccil-netting-eligibility-key]] or [[bilateral-netting-eligibility]].

GIVE UP CLIENTS require the same SWIFT BIC. NON GIVE UP clients require the same FMID. For both client categories, candidate cashflows must have the same currency, SCB Entity, value date, settlement method, and product at CFI-code level.

A DVP deal is excluded from netting. Cashflows generated by Split are eligible.

The source does not specify identifier ownership, normalization, missing-value behavior, CFI comparison precision beyond “CFI code,” or eligibility revalidation timing.
