# ND Delivery-Currency Cashflow Model

The ND delivery-currency cashflow model describes how non-deliverable Rates products produce settlement cashflows in the deliverable currency.

The source states that Cortex delivery events will be sent in delivery currency and that subsequent cashflows will be created. It leaves open whether the conversion is represented within the same trade or through a separate conversion trade, including for ND CCS and ND convert flows.
