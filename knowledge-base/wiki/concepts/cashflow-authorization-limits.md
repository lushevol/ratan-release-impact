# Cashflow Authorization Limits

Cashflow authorization limits are delegated approval thresholds configured in RATAN One in line with RAZOR. Their purpose is to allow users to approve cashflows within their delegated authority.

The guide states that these limits are normally stable during BAU. New user profiles may be configured and existing limits may be amended when required. Any new or amended rule must be tested in UAT before being updated in production.

The source does not provide the actual thresholds, user-to-limit mapping, approval roles, or entitlement matrix.
