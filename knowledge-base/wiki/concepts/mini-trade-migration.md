# Mini Trade Migration

A mini trade migration is a limited-scope migration of selected trades rather than an entire portfolio. The source describes more than 100 trades and records 130 trades on 2026-08-03, but does not confirm that this was the final population.

The scope is represented by paired portfolio identifiers and later by cancelled or original trade-ID lists. The transition between these two scope mechanisms is not explained in the runbook.
