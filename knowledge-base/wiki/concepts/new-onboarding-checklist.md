# New Onboarding Checklist

A **new onboarding checklist** is a structured release-readiness artifact used to coordinate the activities required to bring a new participant, scope, system, or operational process into service.

## RATAN context

The source is stored under **Ratan New Onboarding Checklist 2026** and identifies [[chg1011996]] and [[markets-operations-one]]. The available evidence confirms the document's onboarding context but does not provide the checklist's actual tasks or completion state.

## Expected evidence

A complete onboarding checklist would normally identify:

- Scope and business purpose.
- Responsible owners and approvers.
- Technical and operational dependencies.
- Environment and release dates.
- Validation and acceptance evidence.
- Rollback or recovery procedures.
- Handover and post-release monitoring requirements.
