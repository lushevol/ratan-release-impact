# Cashflow Status Handling

## Definition

Cashflow status handling is the controlled use of status-specific operational actions during settlement processing.

## Status-specific actions

The runbook distinguishes two categories:

- `SWIFT_SUPP`: users bulk unsuppress and then reject these cashflows.
- `READY`: users manually perform an early release of these cashflows.

Both categories are also referenced in the May 17 instruction to release cashflows for accounting. The exact sequencing and authorization model are not documented.
