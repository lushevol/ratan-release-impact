# DVP Netting Exclusion

A deal with settlement type DVP must not be moved into netting in the shared Ratan/S2BNG scope.

The source does not define whether DVP is checked at initial selection only or continuously, nor does it specify the handling of a cashflow that becomes DVP after it has entered a netting set.
