# PID Review

## Definition

A PID review is a review activity involving records or findings identified as `PID`. The source does not define the acronym or specify whether it refers to a process, product, issue class, or another record type.

## Source-specific record

The source states that the **2024 PIDs Review** was updated into the [[lwws]] tool.

No review findings, issue identifiers, remediation statuses, owners, due dates, or closure evidence are included. The update activity must not be treated as proof that all PID findings were resolved.
