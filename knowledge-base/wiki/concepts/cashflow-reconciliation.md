# Cashflow Reconciliation

## Definition

Cashflow reconciliation is the comparison of expected and processed cashflow data after publication of a broader dataset.

## Application in the runbook

The runbook schedules whole-data publication on May 10 at 9 AM and assigns the subsequent reconciliation to [[Yang Chen]] and [[Lina Feng]]. The reconciliation uses the same seven-point checklist as the earlier tranche monitoring.

No comparison basis, tolerance, exception workflow, or reconciliation result is documented.
