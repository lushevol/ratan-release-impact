# Confirmation-Match-Based Payment Release

A proposed payment-release approach in which eligible cashflows proceed based on trade-level Confirmation Full Match or Full Affirmation status, rather than mandatory cashflow affirmation.

The target workflow includes email notification in lieu of affirmation and potential S2B NG-based automation. It must retain controlled exceptions and the ability for authorized users to break STP at cashflow or broader configured scopes.

This source does not define approved eligibility rules, regulatory reporting controls, or the client segmentation that would reconcile this proposal with the separately stated continuation of Outlook-based affirmation.
