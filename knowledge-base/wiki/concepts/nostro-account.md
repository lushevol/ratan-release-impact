# NOSTRO Account

A **NOSTRO account** is an account that a bank holds at another financial institution. From the account-owning bank’s perspective, it represents the bank’s own funds held externally; the phrase means “our account.”

## Example

**Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC)** opens a USD account at **Citibank (USA)** for cross-border USD settlement:

- For ICBC, the account is a NOSTRO account.
- For Citibank, the same account is a VOSTRO account because Citibank hosts ICBC’s funds.

## Operational context

Banks may use NOSTRO accounts to access and transfer their own funds held overseas, settle cross-border payments, manage clearing positions, reconcile balances with correspondent institutions, and support foreign-exchange settlement. Actual FX settlement may involve multiple accounts, correspondent banks, local clearing systems, payment-versus-payment arrangements, or internal settlement accounts.

The source does not specify NOSTRO account schemas, journal entries, lifecycle states, reconciliation controls, or RATAN integration behavior. Those details require authoritative system documentation.
