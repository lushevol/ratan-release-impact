# RATAN SLA Capacity Baseline

The RATAN capacity plan states a current daily-volume baseline of **335,771** items.

| Business process | Daily volume |
|---|---:|
| Trade | 158,263 |
| Settlemet GDC | 173,610 |
| ISO Korea | 3,898 |
| Sum | 335,771 |

The component values are arithmetically consistent with the stated total. The source does not define the business unit counted as an item, identify the reporting period, or establish whether the figures are actual volumes, forecasts, SLA commitments, or test inputs.

The source spelling `Settlemet GDC` is preserved. The likely meaning of this label and the authoritative status of the baseline require confirmation through [[what-is-the-authoritative-ratan-capacity-baseline]].
