# Issue Tracking and Technical-Debt Governance

Issue tracking and technical-debt governance is the controlled practice of recording operational issues and enhancements with sufficient information to establish ownership, priority, impact, status, remediation, and verification.

## Evidence in This Source

[[25-cash-settlement-home-page--25-cash-settlement-home-page--22-functional-requirement--28-02-issue-tracking-tech--6o5vl6]] identifies an external BAU Confluence tracker and two sample issue references. It does not document the governing workflow or demonstrate that the tracker contains the minimum information required for effective governance.

## Minimum Evidence Needed

A governed issue record should identify, at minimum:

- a stable issue identifier and scope;
- business and technical impact;
- priority, status, owner, and relevant dates;
- affected systems or processes;
- root cause and remediation decision;
- linked change, release, or incident records where applicable; and
- evidence that the remediation was validated.
