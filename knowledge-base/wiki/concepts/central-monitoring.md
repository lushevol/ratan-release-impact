# Central Monitoring

Central monitoring is the consolidation of operational visibility for services or platforms, commonly incorporating health signals, alerts, dashboards, and escalation processes.

For RATAN, [[chg1021484]] identifies a Central Monitoring go-live-related record. Its actual monitoring scope, control design, tooling, and operating model are not established by the available source metadata.

## Required evidence

To characterize a central-monitoring implementation, source evidence should identify:

- monitored systems, interfaces, and environments;
- telemetry, dashboards, alerts, and threshold definitions;
- alert triage, escalation, and support ownership;
- readiness approvals and production deployment outcome; and
- post-go-live effectiveness measures.

Do not equate the capability with [[itrs]] or [[ratan-itrs-alert-triage]] without explicit documentation.
