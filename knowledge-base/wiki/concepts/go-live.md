# Go-Live

Go-live is the transition of a system, entity, integration, or business capability into production use.

In the context of [[chg1003770]], the source filename identifies a go-live milestone for RATAN Settlement associated with Uber and an all-entity scope. The available evidence does not confirm that the milestone occurred successfully.

## Evidence required for confirmation

A go-live conclusion should be supported by the authoritative change record and production evidence, including the approved implementation status, deployment timing, readiness checks, test results, settlement or reconciliation validation, support ownership, monitoring arrangements, and rollback outcome.
