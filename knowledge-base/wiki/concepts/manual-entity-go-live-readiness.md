# Manual-Entity Go-Live Readiness

Manual-entity go-live readiness is the evidence-backed state in which settlement configuration, operational controls, testing, and release approval are complete for a manual entity.

For the Tranche 2 checklist, readiness should establish:

- approved and deployed Nostro, SWIFT, cutoff, currency, rounding, timezone, and accounting configuration;
- completion or documented exception treatment for `STRATEGIC_FM_LIST` and cashflow suppression;
- UAT signoff evidence;
- CPT and LMS-verification outcomes;
- approved technical and business go-live dates; and
- closure of country-specific dependencies.

A populated configuration register alone is not readiness evidence. The source records blank UAT attachments, blank technical dates, and incomplete CPT outcomes. See [[what-is-the-approved-tranche-2-manual-entity-go-live-schedule-and-signoff-status]].
