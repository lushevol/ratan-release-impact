# RATAN User-Guide Segmentation

RATAN user guidance is segmented by functional or regional context rather than represented as one universal guide.

The source register identifies:

- **Settlement:** `User manual - RATAN`
- **Korea:** `Ratan One Processing Guide(DOI)-Korea`
- **Trade:** `2-User Guideline`

## Implications

The segmentation suggests that operating procedures may differ between Settlement, Korea, and Trade workflows. Existing wiki material reflects related distinctions in cash settlement, Korea accounting and reconciliation, and trade-information sourcing.

The register does not specify which guide is authoritative for a particular workflow, whether the guides share a common versioning process, or how cross-domain procedures are reconciled.
