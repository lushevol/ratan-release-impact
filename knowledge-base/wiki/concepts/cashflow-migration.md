# Cashflow Migration

Cashflow migration is the transfer of cashflow processing, configuration, data, and operational responsibility between systems.

## 2025 Scope

The roadmap plans migration from [[murex-2-11]] to [[ratan]] across the UK and three geographic tranches.

### Tranche 1

- BANGKOK
- TAIPEI
- OBU TAIPEI
- HONG KONG
- SCS HK

### Tranche 2

- MAURITIUS
- DUBAI
- JAKARTA
- MANILA
- TOKYO
- JOBURG
- PHILIP FCU
- DIFC
- NEWYORK

### Tranche 3

- JERSEY_BR

## Supporting Work

The source shows that migration requires more than data loading. Tranche-related work includes:

- Static-data setup
- Accounting design and implementation
- UAT support
- Reconciliation
- SSI selection hierarchy
- Vostro SI Settlement Means updates
- Dashboard and blotter entity coverage
- Data entitlement

## Planning Uncertainty

Tranches 1 and 2 both carry Q2 2025 targets, while Tranche 3 carries a Q3 target. The source does not document whether the first two tranches are concurrent, sequential, or subject to shared entry criteria.
