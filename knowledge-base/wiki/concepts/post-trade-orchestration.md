# Post-Trade Orchestration

Post-trade orchestration is the coordination of operational processing after trade execution, including the handling of trades, cashflows, events, and exceptions.

[[ratan]] is described as providing this capability for FMO across asset classes. The platform-level claim establishes the intended functional scope but does not assign individual workflows or system-of-record responsibilities to specific services.

Existing services such as [[ratan-cashflow-lifecycle-service]], [[ratan-rule-service]], and [[ratanone-settlement-orchestration-service]] are relevant technical components, but this source does not establish that they collectively or exclusively implement all RATAN post-trade functions.
