# Client Settlement Automation Eligibility

Client settlement automation eligibility is the proposed configuration framework determining whether a cashflow may proceed through NSTP/STP, notification, affirmation, auto-release, or automated netting.

The source proposes classification from SCI as Interbank, FIs, or Corporate; NSTP client tagging; configurable rules by client, product taxonomy, strategy, currency, and structured transaction; netting cut-offs; and maker/checker-maintained contact and template static.

No canonical eligibility model, static-data owner, approval process, or precise override entitlements are specified.
