# Lien-Driven NSTP

Lien-driven NSTP is non-straight-through processing triggered by a lien indicator or lien amount associated with a cashflow.

The utilization-pilot scope describes lien NSTP as dependent on a Murex 2.11 indicator, potentially for SG/IN/MY cashflow migration. The UK/Germany scope specifies that the lien amount drives cashflow NSTP.

The delivery plan lists a Murex 2.11 Lien Indicator for 2024 H2 without a status. This leaves implementation and release status unresolved.
