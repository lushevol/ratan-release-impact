# Murex Payment NSTP Reason Codes

Murex assigns payment STP/NSTP reasons based on counterparty, SSI, strategy, product, fixing, market-operation, entity, and other conditions.

## Interpretation rule

A cashflow can have multiple NSTP reasons. Reason-code counts are occurrences, not unique payments, and must not be interpreted as the percentage of individual cashflows affected by a condition.

For the cited 2022–2023 CN population, the most frequent reason-tag occurrences were `CORP` (28.97%), `NET` (23.41%), `FIXING` (15.12%), `SI(MUL)` (11.55%), and `STRAT` (7.67%).

## Key conditions

- `CORP` identifies a counterparty that is neither Bank nor Internal under Murex counterparty-type and STP-type controls.
- `NET` blocks counterpart-netting-eligible payments from STP. The source identifies `SHACLHO/SHA` as the sole CN counterparty under this rule, but only for the historical analysed population.
- `FIXING` prevents STP of a fixed cashflow while its estimated floating counterpart is awaited for netting.
- `SI` indicates missing nostro or vostro settlement instruction.
- `SI(MUL)` indicates multiple SSI records meeting selection criteria.
- `MOP` concerns cashflows generated by recent or unvalidated market operations.
- `REV` blocks reversal STP when the original is not `SENT` or the market operation is unvalidated.

Historical global reversal evidence reports that 97.74% of reversals were NSTP and that `REV` alone was present on 90.28% of NSTP reversal payments. This does not establish current CN target-state performance.
