# Decision Model and Notation

Decision Model and Notation (DMN) is a standard for modeling operational decisions and decision services. The source describes DMN as XML-based and portable between DMN-compliant platforms, enabling collaboration between business analysts and rule developers.

A Decision Requirements Diagram (DRD) visually represents dependencies within a DMN model. The source identifies Drools DMN runtime support and the DMN Designer in Business Central as differentiating capabilities.

## Project relevance

DMN may be useful where decisions require visual modeling, traceability, and business review. The source does not determine whether Cash Settlement requires DMN, DRL, or both, nor does it define authoring, approval, testing, or deployment controls.
