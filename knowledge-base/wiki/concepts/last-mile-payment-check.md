# Last Mile Payment Check

Last Mile Check is a final-payment inspection mechanism intended to reduce wrong or duplicate payments as RATAN settlement volume and processing complexity increase.

The source states that Phase 1 is live as technical verification only. A proposed Phase 2 business workflow, potentially exposed through Cashflow Blotter or Dashboard actions, remains future work rather than confirmed BAU functionality.

## Boundary

The source does not define the Phase 1 validation rules, failure outcomes, ownership, or a business approval workflow. Operational users should not assume Last Mile Check replaces established queue monitoring, exception resolution, maker/checker controls, or cutoff management.
