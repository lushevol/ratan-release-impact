# Strategic Settlements Platform

A Strategic Settlements Platform is a central platform for settlement workflow, payment generation, accounting, exception handling, business rules, status management, affirmation, and operational reporting.

The source positions RATAN as the Strategic Settlements Platform being built under FMRP. Its intended role extends beyond payment routing: it processes cashflows from legacy and strategic trade populations and coordinates downstream settlement activity.

## Capability scope

The source attributes the following capabilities to RATAN or its surrounding integration model:

- Bilateral, BIC, CCIL, NDS, and IRS netting.
- STP based on TDS3 confirmation status and NSTP based on defined exceptions.
- Payment generation and SWIFT message customisation.
- Settlement accounting through RAZOR, EBBS, and ASPIRE.
- Reversal and rebook exception handling.
- Self-service business-rule maintenance.
- Cashflow and Swift Suppression.
- Real-time exceptions monitoring.
- Cashflow status and sub-status management.
- Single, bulk, and netting affirmation.
- SSDR real-time cashflow extraction and FMMIS volume and touchpoint reporting.

## Status caveat

The source presents a feature catalogue with a `Live` column, but does not provide reliable status values for individual entries. The catalogue should therefore be read as a statement of intended or reported capability, not as proof that every feature is live in production.
