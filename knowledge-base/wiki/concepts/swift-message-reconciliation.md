# SWIFT Message Reconciliation

SWIFT message reconciliation compares messages generated by two systems for equivalent replayed cashflows and classifies each difference. The classifications used in the review are application-logic change, static-data remediation, accepted RATAN behavior, no issue, or unresolved follow-up.

The method is not a requirement for byte-for-byte Murex parity. Murex production replay is a historical comparator, while RATAN behavior may be retained when it is supported by business rules, strategic design, [[entities/razor]] behavior, or static-data interpretation.

## Evidence boundary

A reconciliation disposition does not prove implementation, deployment, or operational readiness. Reliable closure requires a change reference, named owner, effective configuration date, replay-after-remediation evidence, and appropriate payment or compliance validation.

## Main risk

High difference counts may represent shared or inconsistent SSI+ and Nostro static data rather than defects in RATAN logic. Triage must keep those causes separate.
