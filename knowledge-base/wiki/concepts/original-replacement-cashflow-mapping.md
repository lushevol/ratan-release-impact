# Original-to-Replacement Cashflow Mapping

Original-to-replacement cashflow mapping is the proposed RATANONE lineage model for relating a cashflow withdrawn or superseded by an amendment to the replacement cashflow generated by a later major version.

## Model

The mapping records both sides of the relationship across:

- Cashflow identifiers.
- Business versions.
- Cashflow versions.
- Major versions.
- RATAN status and upstream status.
- Upstream source, such as `STELLA` or `MUREX`.
- Active or overdue status.
- Creation and update timestamps.
- A numeric `version` field.

The current mapping is represented by [[entities/ratan-cashflow-mapping]], with historical versions represented by [[entities/ratan-cashflow-mapping-history]].

## Intended use

The mapping supports:

- Linking withdrawal events to replacement cashflows.
- Coordinating amendment workflow.
- Determining when replacement cashflows may reach STP.
- Preserving status and version lineage across cashflow changes.
- Recalling an original cashflow batch when a withdrawal and replacement are proposed to match perfectly.

The “perfect match” recall behavior is explicitly TBD. The source does not define key constraints, matching rules, transaction boundaries, or whether the `version` field is used for optimistic locking, event versioning, or both.
