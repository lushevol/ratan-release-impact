# Cashflow Blotter Filter Rationalization

Cashflow Blotter filter rationalization is the proposed removal of unused or unnecessary filters from the **Custom Search/View** dropdown.

The source establishes the business direction but does not identify any particular filter for removal. The removal inventory must be supplied and approved by the Product Owner.

## Implementation status

Filter removal is blocked pending an authoritative list. Before implementation, the team should establish:

- each filter’s display name and technical identifier;
- the definition and evidence of non-use;
- affected roles, products, and user groups;
- impact on existing saved searches and views;
- business-owner approval; and
- rollback or restoration behavior.

No filter should be classified as unused solely from the screenshot or from this requirement’s general description.

## Boundary

This concept concerns the Cashflow Blotter’s user-interface configuration. It does not imply changes to cashflow lifecycle processing, settlement, accounting, or integration behavior.
