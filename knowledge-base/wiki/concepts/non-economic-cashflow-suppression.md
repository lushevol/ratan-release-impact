# Non-Economic Cashflow Suppression

## Definition

Non-economic cashflow suppression is the control that prevents replacement cashflows created by a non-economic trade amendment from becoming settlement work items in Ratan.

A non-economic amendment changes trade attributes without changing the economic settlement obligation. Stella may nevertheless withdraw cashflows from the prior trade version and create new cashflows for the amended version. Without suppression, those replacement cashflows can appear in the Ratan Cashflow Blotter as `PROJECTED` and create misleading work for Settlement Ops.

## Business Rule

The intended distinction is:

| Amendment type | Replacement cashflows | Ratan treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Non-economic | Generated by Stella but not economically relevant | Suppress or discard from settlement visibility and processing |
| Economic | Economically relevant replacement cashflows | Insert and process through settlement and STP |

Withdrawal events for the prior cashflows may still need to be processed. The source does not establish whether suppressed replacement cashflows are discarded, retained as ineligible records, filtered only from the blotter, or excluded at a later STP stage.

## Processing Flow

1. Stella publishes withdrawal events for cashflows belonging to the previous trade version.
2. Stella publishes replacement cashflow events for the amended trade version.
3. `TDS3` routes the events to `Ratan`.
4. Ratan determines whether the amendment is economically relevant.
5. For a non-economic amendment, Ratan prevents replacement cashflows from creating settlement work.
6. For an economic amendment, Ratan inserts eligible replacement cashflows and allows normal settlement processing.
7. `CDU` and Ratan use the latest trade ID and trade version for confirmation-status and STP coordination.

The settlement-eligibility decision must remain separate from the latest-version rule. Suppression does not mean that confirmation processing should use an older trade version.

## Control Objectives

- Prevent non-economic amendments from generating unnecessary settlement activity.
- Preserve the correct withdrawal and replacement-event lineage.
- Keep confirmation-status notifications aligned with the latest trade version.
- Support audit and reconciliation for suppressed events.
- Avoid treating illustrative IDs such as `C1`–`C6` as canonical identity rules.

## Unresolved Implementation Questions

The source does not specify:

- How the economic classification is conveyed from Stella to Ratan.
- Whether suppression occurs at message receipt, database insertion, blotter presentation, or STP eligibility.
- Whether suppressed cashflows require persistence, audit, or reconciliation records.
- The canonical cashflow-ID mapping when Stella withdraws and recreates cashflows.
- Which system owns `PROJECTED`, `WAITING`, and `SETTLED` transitions.
- Whether the statement that `C3` and `C4` are active after the non-economic amendment is intentional.

These questions are tracked in [[what-is-the-authoritative-ratan-treatment-for-non-economic-amendment-cashflows]].
