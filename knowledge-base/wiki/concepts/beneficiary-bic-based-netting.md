# Beneficiary BIC-Based Netting

Beneficiary BIC-based netting is a UK cash-settlement enhancement in which the beneficiary BIC is used in netting eligibility or grouping decisions.

Ticket **2300350** had function documentation fully ready on 2024-08-16 and was recorded as **In UAT** on 2024-09-20. The source does not provide the detailed eligibility rules, grouping algorithm, UAT outcome, defect status, or production date.

This requirement is distinct from the separately listed UK bilateral-netting item, ticket **4110302**, which was marked out of scope.
