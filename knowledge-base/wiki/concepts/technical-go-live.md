# Technical Go-Live

A technical go-live is the activation or deployment of a system change into a production-capable environment. It is distinct from demonstrating business readiness, completing operational acceptance, or proving sustained production success.

## Evidence expected

A controlled technical go-live normally requires documented evidence of:

- approved scope and deployment timing;
- implementation and rollback procedures;
- technical validation and monitoring;
- identified owners and support arrangements;
- post-deployment checks and any reconciliation required by the affected service.
