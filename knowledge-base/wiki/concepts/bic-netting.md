# BIC Netting

BIC netting is a netting criterion based on the Bank Identifier Code associated with the settlement parties or instructions.

The UK/Germany scope marks BIC netting as included and also requires an auto-netting-table enhancement and netting with up to 500 cashflows.

The SCI dependency plan lists “Ben BIC Netting” for 2024 H2 without a status. The source therefore supports the requirement's presence but not independently verified completion or confirmed ownership.
