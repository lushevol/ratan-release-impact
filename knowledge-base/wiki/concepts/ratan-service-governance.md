# RATAN Service Governance

RATAN service governance is the set of formal records and planning artifacts used to define how the RATAN application is managed as a technology service.

The source register identifies an SLA, an OLA, an `ASRM` record, recovery and restoration plans, a capacity-management plan, and a test-environment specification. Together, these references indicate governance across service commitments, internal operational responsibilities, resilience, resource planning, and controlled testing.

## Evidence boundaries

The source only confirms that these artifacts are linked. It does not provide:

- SLA objectives or service-level measurements
- OLA responsibilities or support boundaries
- Document owners or escalation paths
- Approval status, review dates, or version information
- Recovery objectives or restoration runbooks
- Capacity thresholds or forecasting rules

The linked records must be reviewed before treating any particular policy, target, or responsibility as authoritative.
