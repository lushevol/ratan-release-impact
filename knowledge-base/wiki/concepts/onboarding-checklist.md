# Onboarding Checklist

## Definition

An onboarding checklist is a controlled list of prerequisites, configuration activities, validations, evidence, and approvals used to determine whether a new entity, product, service, or operating scope can be enabled.

The source title indicates that the referenced document uses a checklist format for entity and/or product onboarding. The actual checklist content was unavailable during ingestion.

## Expected control dimensions

A complete onboarding checklist may distinguish:

- Required versus optional activities
- Dependencies and sequencing
- Business, operations, data, and technology ownership
- Test evidence and validation results
- Approval and sign-off requirements
- Production activation criteria
- Remediation, rollback, and post-activation checks

These dimensions are generic characteristics of checklist-based onboarding and are not confirmed requirements of the source.

The checklist may provide operational evidence for [[concepts/static-data-readiness]] or [[concepts/release-readiness-attestation]] if the source explicitly includes reference-data checks, formal approvals, or release gates. Such relationships require confirmation from the document body.
