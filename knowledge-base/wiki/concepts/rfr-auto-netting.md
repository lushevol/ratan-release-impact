# RFR Auto Netting

RFR Auto Netting is the proposed auto-netting capability for risk-free-rate-related cashflows.

ADO ticket **5679760** was recorded on 2024-09-20 as **High level requirement & approach settled**. This status indicates alignment on the high-level requirement and approach; it does not establish that the capability was built, tested, or deployed.

The source links the requirement to the `Auto Netting Features` documentation in the FMRP programme. Detailed eligibility rules, grouping keys, netting calculations, and release controls are not provided.
