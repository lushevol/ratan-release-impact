# Bypass Validation Rule

A Bypass Validation Rule excludes specified entity and product combinations from a validation step. The Tranche 2 checklist proposes bypassing validation for `EG/NP/SAUDI/LOANIQ/CN(FX)` and notes that post-MO validation moved to FMRP.

The checklist marks this item as not required for Tranche 2, but its wording includes uncertainty about whether the move to FMRP makes the rule unnecessary. That status should be confirmed rather than treated as a fully documented design decision.
