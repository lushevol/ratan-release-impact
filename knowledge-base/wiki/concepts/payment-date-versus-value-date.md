# Payment Date versus Value Date

The proposed FMRP flow separates the business date generated by Stella from the operational settlement date maintained by post-trade.

- `Value Date` is populated by Stella and remains unchanged.
- `Payment Date` is added and maintained by Ratan.
- FMO can update `Payment Date` in the Ratan GUI after a failed or delayed process.
- Swift message generation uses the updated `Payment Date` as the settlement date.
- The existing Stella interface for updating `Value Date` is not required in the target flow.

For example, a cashflow may retain `Value Date` `9/30/2022` while `Payment Date` is changed to `10/3/2022` before validation and Swift generation.

The source does not define field type, timezone, nullability, audit requirements, permissions, or post-release mutability.
