# Correspondent Banking

**Correspondent banking** describes arrangements in which one financial institution provides account, payment, clearing, or settlement services to another financial institution.

## Relationship to VOSTRO and NOSTRO accounts

VOSTRO and NOSTRO are reciprocal perspective labels used within correspondent-banking relationships:

- The institution holding another institution’s account describes it as a VOSTRO account.
- The institution whose funds are held externally describes the account as a NOSTRO account.

The labels must be interpreted together with the viewpoint, account ownership, host institution, and currency.

## Operational applications

Correspondent-banking arrangements can support:

- Cross-border transfers and remittances.
- Clearing and reconciliation of balances and settlement positions.
- Foreign-exchange settlement and related currency movements.
- Management of funds held for another financial institution.

The source provides only foundational terminology. It does not define a specific correspondent-banking operating model, accounting treatment, payment message, reconciliation control, or RATAN interface.
