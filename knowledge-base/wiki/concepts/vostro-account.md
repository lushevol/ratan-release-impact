# VOSTRO Account

A **VOSTRO account** is an account held at a bank for another financial institution. From the host bank’s perspective, the account contains funds belonging to the other institution; the phrase means “your account.”

VOSTRO is a perspective label rather than an entirely separate account relationship. The same account is a NOSTRO account from the perspective of the institution that owns the funds and holds the account externally.

## Corrected example

If **Bank B (USA)** opens a USD account at **Bank A (China)**:

- For Bank A, the account is a VOSTRO account.
- For Bank B, the same account is a NOSTRO account.

The source note’s alternative wording—“Bank A opens a USD account for Bank B”—is ambiguous and incorrectly assigns the VOSTRO label to Bank A without clearly distinguishing the account holder’s perspective.

## Operational context

VOSTRO accounts may support correspondent-banking activities such as cross-border transfers, settlement-position management, clearing, reconciliation, and the recording of customer or counterparty funds. The source does not establish specific accounting entries, balance codes, system fields, or RATAN processing rules.
