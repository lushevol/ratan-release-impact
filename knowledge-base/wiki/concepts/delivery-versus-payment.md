# Delivery versus Payment

Delivery versus Payment, or DVP, is a settlement approach that coordinates payment with delivery of the corresponding asset. The 2025 FMRP backlog proposes DVP automation as a Day 2 feature under ADO 6469622.

## Proposed Phases

The source defines three phases:

1. Over Account DVP.
2. Precious Metals DVP via CIS.
3. External Nostro DVP.

The third phase is marked as able to move to 2026. The source therefore does not establish that all three phases formed part of the committed or delivered 2025 scope.

## Related Controls

The External Nostro phase connects DVP automation to [[nostro-vostro-settlement-controls]]. The backlog also references receipt-cashflow controls for Precious Metals and over-account flows, but it does not explicitly state whether those KTLO items are dependencies of DVP automation.

CHATS/PvP is listed separately as a potential Day 2 feature. It should not be treated as part of the DVP implementation without further evidence.
