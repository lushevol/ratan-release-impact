# FEDWIRE SSI Stamping

FEDWIRE SSI stamping is the application of settlement instructions for FEDWIRE processing.

Ticket **5353739** was recorded on 2024-09-20 as **Analysis to be closed by this week**. This status does not establish that analysis actually closed, that implementation began, or that the requirement entered UAT or production.
