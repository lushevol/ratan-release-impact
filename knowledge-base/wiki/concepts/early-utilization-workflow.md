# Early Utilization Workflow

Early Utilization is a required but undefined workflow for FXU market expansion. The source names the workflow without specifying its trigger, eligible products, actors, approvals, state transitions, accounting treatment, or exception handling.

Its design must be coordinated with [[concepts/forward-trade-util-stamping]], the forward-trade utilization window, and the requirement for EBBS entry to be passed on value date.
