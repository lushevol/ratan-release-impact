# Murex-RATAN Cashflow Reconciliation

The delivery plan includes a planned “Murex Cashflow Realtime Recon with RATAN” work item in Q1 2023 Sprint 2.

It also identifies related reconciliation-oriented work: accounting entries posting to CPN suspense for TLM recon and the exclusion of accounting populations from Murex-to-Aspire and Murex-to-[[ebbs]] reporting. Both accounting items depend on [[razor]] accounting design.

The plan provides no reconciliation population, matching keys, frequency, tolerances, exception workflow, reporting specification, or evidence of delivery. “Realtime” should therefore be understood only as a stated planning requirement.
