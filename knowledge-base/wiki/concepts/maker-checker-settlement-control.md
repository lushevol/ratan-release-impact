# Maker/Checker Settlement Control

Maker/checker settlement control is a dual-control workflow requiring one user to initiate or prepare an action and another to affirm or approve it.

The SG/IN/MY/CN Day 2 scope includes pending affirmation, bulk approval, reversal and rebook as maker/checker, and non-economic changes. Bulk approval and maker/checker affirmation are marked complete in the scope section, while the source does not provide test or production evidence.

The control is part of operational settlement governance rather than a netting-calculation rule.
