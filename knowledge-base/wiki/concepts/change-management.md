# Change Management

Change management is the controlled recording, review, approval, implementation, validation, and closure of an operational or production change.

## CHG0875198

`CHG0875198` is a change identifier named in a RATAN release/onboarding file. The available evidence does not establish its status, implementation timing, approval history, scope, affected environment, owners, or outcome.

A change record should distinguish planning material from completed evidence and should retain traceable approval, deployment, validation, and rollback information. In this case, the source body must be reviewed before any such attributes can be recorded.
