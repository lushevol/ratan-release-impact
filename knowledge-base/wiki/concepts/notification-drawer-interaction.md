# Notification Drawer Interaction

Notification drawer interaction is the draft frontend UX scope for presenting notifications in the [[cash-settlement-home-page]].

The named wireframe interactions cover showing a message, viewing notification history, closing the notification drawer, and viewing notification details.

## Detail-view alternatives

The draft includes two alternatives, “View Notification Detail Option 1” and “View Notification Detail Option 2.” It does not choose between them or provide decision criteria such as accessibility, navigation cost, information density, implementation complexity, or audit needs.

## Missing behavior

Because detailed interactions reside in embedded images, the textual design does not establish read/unread state, dismissal behavior, ordering, pagination, retention, history data source, detail authorization, or consistency between real-time delivery and history.
