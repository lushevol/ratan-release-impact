# Email Affirmation Automation

## Definition in the Sources

`Email Affirmation Automation` is listed as an `AI Use Case` in the 2026 Cash Settlement Home Page change index. The more specific 2026 functional-requirement source describes it as a proposed Cash Settlement capability for processing affirmation-related email communications.

The change-index source provides a link to the related Confluence page:

[Email Affirmation Automation (AI Use Case)](https://confluence.global.standardchartered.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=3596370589)

## Available Design Evidence

The available specific source contains headings for background and design flow, including “AI Affirmation Automation Flow,” but does not provide process steps or a functional specification.

The following details remain unspecified:

- The business problem and in-scope affirmation scenarios.
- The automation workflow.
- Email ingestion, classification, extraction, validation, and data-input behavior.
- The authoritative destination system for affirmation data.
- Whether the capability is advisory, routes work for review, drafts communications, or performs automated updates.
- Confidence thresholds, human-review routing, exception handling, audit controls, users, and approval status.
- Implementation status, model selection, accuracy results, production deployment, and automated-action authority.

## Evidence Boundary

Neither source establishes an implemented AI capability. The label `AI Use Case` and the presence of an “AI Affirmation Automation Flow” heading should not be interpreted as evidence of an implemented AI model, production deployment, defined automation behavior, or authority to perform automated actions.
