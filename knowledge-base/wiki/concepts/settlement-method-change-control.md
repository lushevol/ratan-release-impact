# Settlement Method Change Control

Settlement method change control governs the stamping of a settlement method before trade processing and controlled transitions between methods such as `CLS`, `NET`, `GROSS`, `DVP`, and `BOE`.

The source records email approval from TRM for current-state transitions to Gross and proposes a HORIZON approval workflow. It also requires a golden source for settlement-method values and eligibility criteria.

Whether this capability is required for China Day 1 is explicitly `TBC`.
