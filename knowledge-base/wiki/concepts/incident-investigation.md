# Incident Investigation

Incident investigation is the process of examining a production incident to establish what happened, determine contributing or root causes, assess impact, and identify corrective action.

## Evidence in the Source

The Ratan incident-list template includes an `Investigation Details` field. This indicates that investigation findings are intended to be recorded alongside the issue description and incident number.

No investigation details are populated in the current source. The document therefore provides no evidence about causes, contributing conditions, affected services, or investigative outcomes for any Ratan incident.

## Expected Investigation Record

A complete investigation record would normally identify the incident, describe the observed behavior and impact, document the analysis and root cause, and connect the findings to a corrective or preventive fix. Fix implementation and validation should be recorded separately where applicable, including links to related problem, change, or post-incident records.
