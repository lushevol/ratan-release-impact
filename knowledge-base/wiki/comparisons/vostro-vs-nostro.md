# VOSTRO vs NOSTRO

VOSTRO and NOSTRO describe the same correspondent-account relationship from opposite institutional perspectives.

| Dimension | VOSTRO | NOSTRO |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | “Your account” | “Our account” |
| Viewpoint | The bank hosting another institution’s funds | The institution that owns funds held at another bank |
| Account location | At the describing bank | At another financial institution |
| Example | Citibank’s view of ICBC’s USD account at Citibank | ICBC’s view of its USD account at Citibank |
| Typical context | Holding or recording another institution’s funds | Accessing and settling the bank’s own externally held funds |

## Key rule

Classification depends on perspective, not solely on the account or currency. An account can be:

- **NOSTRO** for the institution that owns the funds and maintains the account at another bank.
- **VOSTRO** for the institution that hosts the account and holds the other institution’s funds.

## Corrected Bank A/Bank B example

If Bank B (USA) opens a USD account at Bank A (China), the account is VOSTRO from Bank A’s perspective and NOSTRO from Bank B’s perspective. This corrects the source note’s ambiguous first example.
