# Prime Trade Migration

Prime Trade Migration is a Strategic Cash Settlements workstream covering Prime trade migration into RATAN, trade settlement-instruction stamping, PS4 migration, and associated FIT and UAT support.

## Scope

The tracker identifies:

- RATAN Trade SI Stamping for Prime trades;
- PS4 Prime Trade Migration;
- Prime UAT support from RAZOR and FMSGW;
- investigation of Prime new events and CPN analysis;
- settlement-method analysis.

The source does not define the migration population, cutover plan, acceptance criteria, or accountable owners. The relationship to [[ssi-stamping-hierarchy]] should be validated against the detailed Prime design and migration documentation.
