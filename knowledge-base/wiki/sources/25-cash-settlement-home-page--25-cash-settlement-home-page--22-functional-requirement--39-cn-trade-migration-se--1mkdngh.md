# CN Trade Migration - Settlement Process

This functional requirement proposes settlement controls for migrating CN trades from [[murex-2-11]] to [[stella]], with [[ratan]] processing post-migration cashflows.

The document assumes a final Murex business day of 10 May 2024, migration activity on 11–12 May, and post-migration Stella-to-Ratan FMRP processing from 13 May. These dates are planning assumptions, not evidence that the cutover occurred on those dates.

## Main proposal

For cashflows with a value date on or after 13 May that were settled early in Murex 2.11 before the migration weekend, the preferred option is a batch status-only update of equivalent Stella cashflows from `PROJECTED` to `SETTLED`.

The update is required to:

- take a programme-provided list of cashflow IDs as a dynamic input;
- represent settlement already completed through Murex 2.11;
- prevent duplicate payments from the rebooked Stella cashflows;
- send no message to [[razor]];
- generate no SWIFT message; and
- allow subsequent Stella amendments to follow ordinary Ratan withdrawal, rebooking, and netting behaviour.

The source describes this as a preferred functional option, not as confirmed implementation, approval, UAT completion, or production behaviour.

## Cutover value-date rules

| Period or value date | Intended settlement or migration handling |
| --- | --- |
| VD `<= 10 May` | Settle through Murex 2.11 → Ratan; complete by EOD 10 May. |
| 11–12 May | Perform migration activities. |
| VD `>= 13 May` | Process through Stella → Ratan FMRP after the migration weekend. |
| VD 13 May settled on 10 May | Treat as an early-settlement exception requiring overlap handling. |

A Stella migrated trade is intended to have an effective date of 12 May. Later FO/MO market-event updates may only move that date forward. Aggregation cashflows are intended to use VD 11 May; treatment of earlier historical aggregate dates remains unresolved.

## Options for early-settled overlaps

| Option | Pros | Cons | Source position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No early settlement | Clean settlement and LMS forecast; no new function | Requires weekend [[settlement-ops]] support; client agreement to delay payments; high volume of VD-1 client requests | Not preferred; source asks whether to rule it out. |
| Suppress Stella overlap cashflows | No new function | Subsequent amendments may break; duplicate-payment risk; hard block does not work; manual settlement in [[oscar]]; LMS dependency | Not preferred. |
| Set Stella overlap cashflows as `SETTLED` | Hard block works; subsequent amendments work normally | Requires a status-update function; individual recall and resettlement may require Oscar; LMS dependency | **Preferred option**. |

| Mapping field | Meaning |

Cashflows within a mapped trade are compared by:

| Matching attribute |
| --- |
| Entity (`FMID`) |
| Counterpart (`FMID`) |
| Payment Date |
| Currency |

The source conflicts on the reconciliation horizon: the initial requirement says “next 9 days,” while the final reconciliation section specifies payment-date scope `T+7`.

## Temporary NSTP and suppression rules

| Rule | Condition or scope |
| --- | --- |
| Aggregated Stella cashflow suppression | Payment-type prefix `Migrated_Aggregated_`; an earlier requirement describes `Cashflow Status == 'SUSPENDED'`. |
| SCF booking suppression | Cashflows for SCF booking of Murex 2.11 dead trades. |
| Murex reversal control | Hold reversal cashflows created by migration-weekend trade cancellation. An earlier section instead requires suppression. |
| Stella new-booking NSTP | Hold newly booked Stella cashflows as NSTP; possible condition `Data_Flow.Data_Sender=='STELLA'`. |

The Murex reversal rule is intended to be removed after migration activities. The source does not define removal criteria, ownership, or the authoritative terminal status.

## Open control issues

- The required reconciliation scope is inconsistent: next 9 days versus `T+7`.
- Murex reversal handling conflicts between `SUPPRESSED` and NSTP hold.
- The batch update lacks defined authorization, idempotency, audit fields, reason codes, rollback, and eligibility controls.
- The source explicitly leaves Stella trade confirmation status as TBC.
- [[lms]] feed and balance treatment is unresolved when a Stella cashflow is marked `SETTLED` without a current Ratan/Razor payment.
- The “revived approach” for LMS is not defined.
