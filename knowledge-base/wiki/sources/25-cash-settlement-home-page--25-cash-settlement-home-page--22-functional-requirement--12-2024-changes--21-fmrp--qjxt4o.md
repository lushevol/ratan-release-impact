# Murex and RATAN SWIFT Difference Review

## Purpose and scope

This document records multi-phase reconciliation between SWIFT messages generated from Murex production replays and messages generated by [[entities/ratan]]. The review covers H1 Swift Recon, UK/DE Swift Recon, Tranche 1, and Tranche 2.

The review is a requirements and reconciliation record. It does not by itself prove that every agreed configuration or code change was implemented, deployed, or operationally validated.

## Review phases

- **H1 Swift Recon:** 31 January 2024, 1 February 2024, and 31 May 2024. The replay produced 2,026 difference records: MT103 (13), MT202 (869), MT202Flip (12), MT210 (1,047), MT604 (48), MT605 (34), and MT103/202 COV (3). Participants were Dinesh, Sumita, Pradeesh, and Wayne.
- **UK/DE Swift Recon:** 28 June 2024 and 1 July 2024, reviewed on 9 September 2024. Participants were Dinesh, Pradeesh, Wayne, Sarah, and Kayla.
- **Tranche 1 Swift Recon:** 24 March to 4 April 2025, reviewed on 15 April 2025. Coverage included Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Thailand. Participants were Dinesh, Sumita, Wayne, and Pradeesh.
- **Tranche 2 Swift Recon:** 12 to 23 May 2025, reviewed on 2 July 2025. Coverage included Mauritius, Dubai, DIFC, Jakarta, Manila, Philippines FCU, Tokyo, and Johannesburg. Participants were Dinesh, Sumita, and Pradeesh.

## Reconciliation principle

Murex replay output is a comparison baseline, not a universal specification that [[entities/ratan]] must reproduce byte for byte. Differences were classified as:

- RATAN application-logic changes;
- SSI+ or Nostro static-data remediation;
- intentional or strategically accepted RATAN behavior;
- formatting or message-content variance requiring no change; or
- unresolved follow-up.

The disposition should therefore distinguish a functional defect from a difference caused by static data or an explicitly accepted design choice.

## Confirmed RATAN changes

The source explicitly records the following actions:

- Remove Block 3 tag `121` for MT210 while retaining the RATAN Block 3 logic.
- Remove tag `52` from MT202.
- Change the Singapore entity tag 53/58 BIC to `SCBLSG22GMO`.
- For MT202Flip tag `52`, use the beneficiary account number rather than the counterparty FMID as the account-number source.
- For MT103COV tag `59`, concatenate the two account-name fields and split the result into lines of up to 70 characters, ignoring characters beyond 70.
- For MT604 and MT605 Block 2 receiver derivation, copy the specified Murex logic for the blank-`FIN_COPY` path.
- For MT605 tag `82A`, derive the value from the Ordering customer BIC.

The source does not provide implementation status, release identifier, code reference, owner, or test evidence for these actions.

The confirmed action says: `Copy Murex logic with FINCOPY is blank`. It is unclear whether paths (a) and (b) were already supported, excluded from scope, or omitted from the implementation instruction.

## High-volume static-data differences

The largest recorded populations include:

- 886 MT202 differences in field 57A/58A line 1, assigned to SSI-data checking;
- 951 MT210 field 52A line 1 differences, noted as addressed by a Nostro-file update;
- 844 MT202 field 72 line 1 differences, assigned to SSI-data checking;
- 200 MT202 field 58A secondary-line differences, with the RATAN setup retained;
- 250 MT202 field 72 other-line differences, classified as no issue;
- 61 Tranche 2 MT202 messages where Murex did not generate tag 53, classified as expected RATAN behavior;
- 63 Tranche 2 MT202 messages where field 72 line 1 was absent in RATAN, classified as expected RATAN behavior; and
- 36 Tranche 2 MT210 messages with field 52A differences and missing field 56 in RATAN, classified as expected RATAN behavior.

These counts show that reconciliation differences cannot be interpreted directly as application-defect counts. SSI+ and Nostro static data are material determinants of generated SWIFT content.

## Outstanding dependencies

The source leaves the following items open or deferred:

- Confirmation of how special MT604/MT605 field 56/57 accounts are identified for GBP, EUR, and HKD. The noted patterns are `//SC[Account number]` for GBP and `//TR[Account number` for EUR; HKD remained to be confirmed.
- FEDWIRE `FW` prefixes for field 54 and the first field 57, deferred to a FEDWIRE SSI ADO task.
- Ten Tranche 2 cashflows with field 58A differences, assigned to Sumita and Pradeesh for SSI+ checking under SSI ID `74663620`.
- South Africa Tranche 2 field 50K and 59 differences, assigned to Pradeesh for production-value checking.
- H2 addition or final disposition of MT210 tags 25 and 56.

## Evidence limitations

The record contains named reviewers, sample cashflows, counts, and dispositions, but generally does not include effective dates for static-data changes, implementation references, deployment evidence, replay-after-remediation results, payment-risk assessment, or compliance approval. “No change required” and “expected behavior” should therefore be treated as review dispositions rather than complete release evidence.
