# Sri Lanka SCB Colombo CMB Hands-on Training Use Cases

This source is a hands-on training use-case checklist for [[sri-lanka-scb-colombo-cmb-in-country]] within Settlement Day 2 manual-entity settlement enablement.

## Listed use cases

1. SI Input
2. Netting
3. Swift Suppression
4. Cashflow Suppression
5. Early Release
6. Un-net
7. Split
8. Un-Split

## Scope and evidence

The checklist directly establishes that these eight scenarios were selected for training in the named Sri Lankan in-country entity scope. It does not contain test steps, input data, expected results, outcomes, approvals, or pass/fail evidence.

The separate entries for Swift Suppression and Cashflow Suppression indicate that they are treated as distinct training scenarios, but the source does not define their relationship. Netting and Un-net, and Split and Un-Split, are paired scenarios, but reversibility conditions and downstream effects are not specified.

This document must not be treated as evidence of completed or passed UAT. It is related to [[country-specific-settlement-uat-coverage]] and the broader [[uat-test-case]] framework.
