## Evidence and limitations

The directory supports the existence and intended role of these linked resources. It does not establish their current approval status, owners, review dates, accessibility, implementation status, or technical content.

The listed recovery procedure is explicitly temporary and versioned `v1.0`; it must not be treated as a confirmed target-state recovery model. The `RATAN Portal - Dev` entry has no address, so its access path and operational status are unknown.

The `RATAN NFA` title associates `51358` with global non-functional requirements for 2025, but does not define whether `51358` is an interface, project, delivery, or other governance reference.
