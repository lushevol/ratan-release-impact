# Cashflow Events Control

This functional requirement defines how [[ratan]] receives and controls cashflows from target-state [[stella]] and interim [[murex-211]]. It establishes two controls: batch-level gating and ordering for all cashflows generated by one trade event, followed by per-cashflow event sequencing for `New` and `Withdrawal` messages.

The design positions Ratan as a source-agnostic settlement platform. Stella is the intended trade lifecycle engine and cashflow generator, while Murex 2.11 is an interim source whose differing behaviour requires an adaptor layer before its cashflows conform to the strategic model.

## Principal requirements

- A batch must remain unavailable to the blotter and STP/NSTP processing until every expected member has arrived.
- Batches for one trade must be dispatched in ascending batch order. A later complete batch waits for every member of an earlier batch to be sent to STP/NSTP.
- For one cashflow ID, `New → Withdrawal` is normal; `New → Withdrawal → New` can represent Undo/Revive; repeated `New` events retain only the first event.
- A previously released or settled cashflow is the primary threshold for reversal and rebook controls following amendments, termination, novation, and cancellation.
- Netting and split controls distinguish unsettled structures, which may be automatically unwound, from released or settled structures, which require controlled manual processing.
- Undo after a settled cancellation/reversal creates duplicate-payment risk and should be blocked or treated as an exception.
- Expiry events and their corresponding revive messages are non-economic and should be discarded.

## Batch identity and source alignment

Stella provides an explicit packed `Cashflow_Sequence` value in the format:

```text
BatchId_Sequence_Count
```

Example:

```text
1_1_2
```

|  | Trade ID | Major Version | Batch ID | Cashflow Sequence(Within one batch) | Count |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stella Fields | Trade_Id(DM) | Cashflow.Cashflow_Major_Version(DM) | Cashflow_Sequence(DM) | Cashflow_Sequence(DM) | Cashflow_Sequence(DM) |
| Murex 2.11 | /MxPayML/scbExtraInfoBlock/TrnOrginalID | Rule_Murex_Batch_Management | Rule_Murex_Batch_Management | Rule_Murex_Batch_Management | Rule_Murex_Batch_Management |

Murex 2.11 has no equivalent explicit source field. Ratan is expected to infer a batch from the cumulative `<Flows>` snapshot supplied in payment messages, preserve a batch audit, and derive major version, batch ID, intra-batch sequence, and count.

## Unresolved matters

The document does not define missing-batch timeout or recovery handling, a formal Murex `<Flows>` comparison algorithm, duplicate-detection keys, or the authoritative Undo state-restoration method. It also contains a tension between general Drop 2/Drop 3 NSTP handling for released cashflows and a China Day 1 statement that cancellation reversals can STP.
