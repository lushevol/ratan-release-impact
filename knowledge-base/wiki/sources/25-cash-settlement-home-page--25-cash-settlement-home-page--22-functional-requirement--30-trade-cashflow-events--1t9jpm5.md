# Ratan Non-Economic Cashflow Handling

## Summary

This functional requirement addresses BAU trade amendments that change non-economic attributes. Under the described Stella behavior, a non-economic amendment withdraws the cashflows from the previous trade version and creates replacement cashflows for the amended version. These events are sent through `TDS3` to `Ratan`.

The proposed control is to discard or suppress replacement cashflows generated solely by a non-economic amendment so that they do not appear as settlement work items for Settlement Ops. Replacement cashflows from an economic amendment remain eligible for insertion into Ratan and settlement processing.

The requirement also states that `CDU` uses the latest trade ID and trade version for confirmation-status notifications regardless of whether the amendment is economic or non-economic. Trade-version synchronization and cashflow settlement eligibility are therefore separate but coordinated controls.

## Stella Cashflow Generation

The source uses the following illustrative event sequence:

| FO/MO Action | Trade Event | Trade Action | Trade Id | Trade Major Version | Cashflow ID | Cashflow Event |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New Trade Booking | Trade | Book | T1 | V1 | C1 | New |
| New Trade Booking | Trade | Book | T1 | V1 | C2 | New |
| Non Economic Amendment | Trade | Update | T1 | V2 | C1 | Withdrawal |
| Non Economic Amendment | Trade | Update | T1 | V2 | C2 | Withdrawal |
| Non Economic Amendment | Trade | Update | T1 | V2 | C3 | New |
| Non Economic Amendment | Trade | Update | T1 | V2 | C4 | New |

All cashflow IDs and events are described as flowing from Stella through `TDS3` to `Ratan`.

## Problem Statement

The source identifies `C3` and `C4` as replacement cashflows generated by a non-economic amendment. When inserted into the Ratan Cashflow Blotter, they appear with `PROJECTED` status. Settlement Ops could therefore interpret them as requiring settlement activity even though the amendment should not change economic settlement.

The intended operational outcome is that non-economic amendment cashflows do not create settlement activity or visible settlement work for Settlement Ops.

## Proposed Treatment

The proposed solution is:

- Process withdrawal events for the previous cashflows as required.
- Discard or suppress `C3` and `C4`, the replacement cashflows generated by the non-economic amendment.
- Do not expose those replacement cashflows in the settlement blotter.
- Continue normal settlement processing for replacement cashflows generated by an economic amendment.

The illustrative economic amendment creates `C5` and `C6` as new cashflows after withdrawing earlier cashflows. Those new cashflows remain `PROJECTED` and are intended to proceed through settlement.

## Integration with Stella and CDU

The requirement states that Ratan updates the status of active cashflows and that `CDU` always uses the latest trade ID and trade version for confirmation-status notification:

| Trade Scenario | Trade ID | Trade Version | Expected CDU Confirmation Identifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Trade Booking | T1 | V1 | T1 + V1 |
| Trade Non Economic Amendment | T1 | V2 | T1 + V2 |
| Trade Economic Amendment | T1 | V3 | T1 + V3 |

The latest-version principle applies to both economic and non-economic amendments. Ratan is expected to follow this principle when driving cashflow STP.

The source also contains an example in which `C3` and `C4` move from `PROJECTED` to `PROJECTED->SETTELD`. This conflicts with the proposed suppression of those cashflows and should not be treated as an authoritative status transition until clarified.

## Evidence and Limitations

The requirement provides a concrete event sequence and clearly expresses the business objective: non-economic amendments must not create unnecessary settlement activity. It also distinguishes non-economic amendments from economic amendments and establishes the latest trade-version rule for CDU notifications.

The document does not define:

- The authoritative field or event attribute identifying an economic or non-economic amendment.
- Whether suppression occurs on receipt, insertion, blotter display, or STP eligibility.
- Whether withdrawn and suppressed cashflows must be retained for audit and reconciliation.
- The canonical mapping between Stella cashflow IDs and Ratan cashflow records.
- Ownership of `PROJECTED`, `WAITING`, and `SETTLED` status transitions.
- Handling of late, duplicated, or out-of-order amendment events.

The example cashflow IDs `C1`–`C6` are illustrative and should not be interpreted as canonical identifiers.
