### **Description **

** **

**For Scbml：

Cashflow count check **
For cashflow not fully received yet or cashflow pending trade validation,  as requested by settlement ops , these cashflows should not be visible for them to view

***Cashflow pending trade validation***
Cashflow generated by Stella/Murex 2.11 and sent to RATAN, these cashflows can be from trade which are not validated yet.
When received these cashflows, Ratan would check
  1.  Trade state in cashflow, if it is not in validated status,
  2.  RATAN will check the parent trade validation status.
If the trade is not validated,  RATAN will hold these cashflows in the settlement process, cashflows won't be visible from GUI for settlement ops. Trade validation process driven by MO and trade validation status would be sync to TDS3. RATAN then would take the trade validate status from TDS3 and lookup the impact cashflows to resume the settlement process.

**If cashflow is manual touched(comment), based on the information below to identify if it is economic /non economic change , non economic cashflow would not be processed further.
