# Get Started

1. Open Blade from manifest or desktop start icon.

![image-2025-12-1_12-56-11.png](attachments/image-2025-12-1_12-56-11.png)

2. Open workspace for trade search. Click on Trade Query.

![image-2025-12-1_12-56-27.png](attachments/image-2025-12-1_12-56-27.png)

3. search for trade id equals to specific id.

![image-2025-12-1_12-56-39.png](attachments/image-2025-12-1_12-56-39.png)

4. right click to get menus, click on "Show Cashflow in Ratan".

![image-2025-12-1_12-56-48.png](attachments/image-2025-12-1_12-56-48.png)

5. Get Ratan in a new window. Sign in with your account.

![image-2025-12-1_12-57-12.png](attachments/image-2025-12-1_12-57-12.png)

6. The cashflow blotter will be opened automatically.

![image-2025-12-1_12-57-22.png](attachments/image-2025-12-1_12-57-22.png)

7. the cashflows of trade will be displayed with remaining amount.

![image-2025-12-1_12-57-27.png](attachments/image-2025-12-1_12-57-27.png)
