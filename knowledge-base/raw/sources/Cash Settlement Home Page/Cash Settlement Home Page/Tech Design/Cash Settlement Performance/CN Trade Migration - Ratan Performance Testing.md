# Background

With CN cash settlement has been roll out and run smoothly on production, we are able to foresee that more and more entities will be onboarding to our strategic cash settlement flow in the near future, and of course there will be a larger inflow of data. The stability, accuracy will face greater challenges. In view of this, we need to quantify the capabilities of our system by conducting performance & stress tests including but not limited to throughput, number of concurrencies, response time, resource utilization, network performance, etc.

Group management is the first step to handle with the inbound cashflows, with should not be the bottleneck of whole workflow, so the purpose of this page is to record the performance tuning process and comparison before and after performance optimization.

Please be noted that this page will be kept updated with the PT methodology continues to evolve. The draft version will be very simple to demonstrate the current performance.
