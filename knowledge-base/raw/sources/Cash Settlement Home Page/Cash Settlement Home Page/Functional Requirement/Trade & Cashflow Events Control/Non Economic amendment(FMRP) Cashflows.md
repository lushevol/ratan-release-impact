# FMRP Trade & Cashflow handling agreement

- Stella will do generate new trade version for the non eco trade amendment
- Stella will withdrawal the cashflows of last trade version(before the trade amendment) and generate new cashflows for the new trade version( post trade amendment) E.g there're C1, C2 from new trade booking & C3, C4 from trade non economic amendment, the cashflow events would be as below. 1. Withdrawal C1, C2 2. New C3, C4
- Ratan will compare the withdrawal cashflows C1, C2 with the new cashflows C3, C4 and decide if it's non economic trade amendment. If the below attributes are the same between the withdrawal & new cashflows. 1. 'Booking Entity'(FMID) 2. 'Counterparty'(FMID) 3. Payment Currency 4. Payment Amount 5. Payment Value Date 6. Receive&Pay Direction
- Ratan will ignore the C3, C4 and make C1, C2 available for settlement ops only
- Cashflow Status updating back to Stella: Ratan will update the latest cashflow status to Stella for C3, C4 1. C1, C2 is 'Released'/'Settled'/'Netted' + non eco amendment: update 'Released'/'Settled'/'Netted' to C3, C4 2. C1, C2 is in 'Projected'+ non eco amendment: Later 'Released'/'Settled'/'Netted' status update on C3, C4, C1, C2 will remains as 'Projected' only.

# Ratan Donwstream impact

If there're C1, C2 from new trade booking & C3, C4 from trade non economic amendment, the behavior between Ratan & downstream would be as below.

- C1, C2 will be sent to Razor to generate swift message and Razor send these to FMSRE/AMH, C3 & C4 won't be sent to Razor
- C1, C2 will be sent to LMS for liquidity management and C3, C4 won't.
- C1, C2 will be available in the cashflow API exposed to Ratan EOD, C3 & C4 won't be available for Ratan EOD.

Confirmed with Aspire & TLM team there's no impact to the accounting & recon process.
