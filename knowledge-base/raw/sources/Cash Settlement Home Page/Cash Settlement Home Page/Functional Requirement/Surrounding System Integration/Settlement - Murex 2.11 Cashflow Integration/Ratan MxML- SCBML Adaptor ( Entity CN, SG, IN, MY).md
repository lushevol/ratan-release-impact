# Murex & Ratan Feeding agreement

- Murex 2.11 will feed the next 9 days( calendar days not considering the holiday/weekend) to Ratan. - For existing cashflows, Murex schedule regular job from Monday to Friday and send the next 9 days cashflows to Ratan - For the new booking, if the VD is falling in the next 9 days Murex will send the cashflow to Ratan immediately in real time
- Only the INIT cashflows would be sent to Ratan, Murex payment status would be moved from INIT to SNTR after the message sent to Ratan. Ratan will send ack message to Murex 2.11.
- After settlement ops settle the cashflows in Ratan, Ratan will update the RELEASED status back to Murex and Murex 2.11 update the payment status from SNTR to RSLR. The Ratan SETTLED status won't be sync back to Murex 2.11.
- Murex 2.11 auto netting: Murex 2.11 will do the auto netting for the 2 legs payment for the swap products( e.g. IRS, COM Swap) 1. Murex 2.11 generate the fix leg in advance( VD -9) 2. When Murex 2.11 do the fixing( normally on VD-2) , Murex 2.11 will generate 2 payments (one is reversal of original fix leg payment and the other is the netted amount between fix leg & floating leg payment) and sent to Ratan
- Trade market events & payments: 1. RPL_M/RPL/MOD: Reversal & New Payments 2. RPL_D: Reversal payment
- Non Economic - Murex 2.11 UDF change won't generate reversal & new payments - Murex 2.11 portfolio change within the same entity won't generate reversal & new payments
- SSI refresh job: There's daily schedule job in Murex 2.11 which will check the SSI and generate reversal & new payments in case there's any SSI update. - Daily scheduled job - Global job to cover all the entities trades scan

# Murex 'Flows' info & Batch handling

There're some cases multi payments generated from one trade market event( new trade booking, C&R, Restructure & etc), accordingly Murex 2.11 will publish multi MxML to Ratan.
Ratan need to recognize how many payments MxML sent from one market event and group these payment to monitor no message missing in this interface. e.g Murex 2.11 sent 3 payments from new trade booking but Ratan only receive 2 MxML messages then Ratan need to raise as exception.

Within each of payment MxML, there's a block 'Flows' which carry all the payments generated from the same parent trade. E.g. in the payment message of **94923498 **we can see the other payments.

<Flows>
  <flow>Flowid:**94923494**, status:SNTR, value_date:20230920</flow>
  <flow>Flowid:94923495, status:INIT, value_date:20230920</flow>
  <flow>Flowid:94923496, status:INIT, value_date:20230921</flow>
  <flow>Flowid:94923497, status:INIT, value_date:20231020</flow>
  <flow>Flowid:94923498, status:INIT, value_date:20231020</flow>
</flows>

With the above 'Flows' information, Ratan can get some clue how many payment MxML messages Murex 2.11 may generated & sent to Ratan.

Below is the flow how Ratan interpret the MxML block 'Flows' and identify the payments Ratan suppose receive as a group.

# Post Released/Settled Trade 'Amendment' - NSTP

When there's trade 'Amendment' on the settled cashflow, Murex 2.11 will generate 'Reversal' **94923579 **(to offset the original cashflow) & 'Rebook' **94923580**(the latest cashflow with updated eco info). As requested by PO/Settlement ops, Ratan would hold the Reversal & Rebook cashflow as NSTP.
**There's technical limitation from Murex 2.11**: We can identify the reversal payment **94923579 **but don't have indicator to identify the rebook payment **94923580**. One rule had agreed for CN Day 1 to recognize the rebook cashflow.

- The reversal payment from trade amendment would be NSTP, like the payment 94923579
- The new payment which VD is in the next 30 days after the reversal cashflow would be NSTP like the payment 94923580 and 94923581.
- The new payment which VD is out of next 30 days window of the reversal cashflow would be STP, like the payment 94923582.
