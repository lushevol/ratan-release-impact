# Cutover Date

Assume the 10th May(Fri) 2024 is the cutover date which is the last business day prior to the migration, the migration would happen during the weekend 11th May - 12th May.

- Cashflow with VD <= 10th May suppose be settled by Murex 2.11 → Ratan cashflow settlement flow, settlement should be completed by EOD 10th May.
- Migration happen on 11th May & 12th May weekend.
- Cashflow with VD >= 13th May suppose be settled by Stella → Ratan FMRP flow post the migration weekend.
- There would be special case which some clients request to settle the VD 13th May cashflows on 10th May. ![image2023-10-26_12-18-31.png](attachments/image2023-10-26_12-18-31.png)

# Effective Date of Stella Migration Trade

This would be set as **12th May (Sunday)** for the Stella new booked trade.

- If there's trade market event performed on the migrated trade, the cashflow with VD < effective date won't be impacted. Means the only the Stella cashflows with VD 13th May onward would be impacted by the BAU trade market event.
- If FO/MO wants to update the effective date(as part of market event operation）, the new effective date can only be later than the previous effective date.

# Aggregation Cashflow VD

Would be **11th May (Sat)**, which won't be touched by Stella BAU market event (post trade migration weekend). Is there any requirement to update the past value date e.g. 8th May cashflow( which were aggregated in the migration weekend).

# Problem Statement

- Duplicate payment risk: #2 cashflows( above table) had been settled by the Murex 2.11 cashflows on 10th May but same cashflow(#5 from above table) would be rebook by Stella as part of migration.
- Hard block on the Stella migrated trade: Stella would generate the cashflow(#5) status as 'PROJECTED' which there's no hard block with this status for FO, but actually this cashflow had been settled with Murex generated data(#2).
- Sub sequent market event: Still on the #(5) cashflow, below is one sample of the trade amendment in the post migration BAU. 1. Trade amendment on #5 cashflow : Settle the new updated cashflow only. 2. Trade amendment on normal settled cashflow: Reversal and Rebook handled by settlement ops.
